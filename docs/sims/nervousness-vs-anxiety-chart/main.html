How Clinical Anxiety Differs From Everyday Nervousness

Hover a bar for what each level means · tap a bar to pin a fuller explanation.
Almost everyone feels nervous sometimes — before a test, a game, or a first day. Clinical anxiety is different: it lasts longer, feels more intense, and gets in the way of everyday life. It is a real medical condition that can be treated, not a personal weakness.
