## Specification

The full specification below is extracted from

```text
Type: map
**sim-id:** world-traditions-of-movement-map<br/>
**Library:** Leaflet<br/>
**Status:** Specified

Bloom Taxonomy: Understand (L2)
Bloom Taxonomy Verb: identify, describe, exemplify

Learning objective: Students identify and describe cultural physical activity traditions from different regions of the world, recognizing them as valid forms of physical activity tied to community identity.

Geographic scope: World map with 6 marked regions

Purpose: Show that cultural physical activity traditions exist across the globe and are practiced with pride, not treated as "old-fashioned" or lesser than organized sports

Locations and markers:
- North America (Great Plains/Eastern Woodlands): Powwow dance traditions and the origins of lacrosse
- West Africa: Traditional dance forms tied to community celebrations and drumming
- Ireland: Step dancing traditions
- China: Tai Chi as a traditional movement practice
- Brazil: Capoeira, blending movement, music, and history
- Caribbean: Traditional jump-rope and rhyming games passed through generations

Interactive features:
- Click a marker to open an infobox with a respectful description of the tradition, what it involves physically, and its cultural significance
- Hover over a marker to see the tradition's name and region before clicking

Legend: Simple icon per tradition type (dance, sport, game, martial art)

Color scheme: Warm, neutral map palette; markers in a single friendly accent color so no region is visually emphasized over another

Responsive behavior: Map resizes to container width; markers remain clickable at all screen sizes; on narrow screens, marker list becomes a scrollable panel below the map

Implementation: Leaflet.js with a static base map and custom marker popups. All descriptions written respectfully, describing traditions as valid ongoing practices rather than historical curiosities.
```
