```text
Type: infographic
**sim-id:** body-safety-rules-interactive-guide<br/>
**Library:** p5.js<br/>
**Status:** Specified

Bloom Taxonomy: Understand (L2)
Bloom Taxonomy Verb: explain, describe, recognize

Learning objective: Students explain the three core body-safety rules (my body belongs to me, I can say no, I always tell a trusted adult) by exploring a calm, simple interactive guide.

Purpose: Reinforce the three body-safety rules in a warm, non-frightening way that a Grade 1 student and teacher can revisit together

Layout: Three large, friendly panels arranged side by side, each with a simple icon: a heart over a body outline ("My body belongs to me"), a raised hand ("I can say no"), and a trusted adult figure with a speech bubble ("I always tell a trusted adult")

Interactive elements:
- Click each panel to reveal one calm sentence explaining the rule in simple words
- Click a fourth "What if?" panel to reveal: "If a touch ever feels confusing or wrong, remember: it is never your fault, and telling a trusted adult is always the right choice — you will not be in trouble for telling."
- No panel contains frightening imagery; all illustrations are calm, warm, and reassuring

Visual style: Soft, warm color palette; simple flat-style icons; generously spaced panels so the page never feels crowded or intense
Color scheme: Soft blue and green tones throughout, avoiding any alarming colors like harsh red

Implementation: p5.js or HTML/CSS with click-to-reveal panels; each reveal is text-only, no animation, to keep the tone calm and steady

Implementation notes: This infographic must be reviewed for tone before publishing — every sentence should sound reassuring and empowering, never frightening. Teachers may want to pause on the "What if?" panel and read it aloud slowly.
```
