# Quiz: Safety And Trusted Adults

Test your understanding of sharing safely, preventing injuries, safety procedures, and trusted adults with these review questions.

---

#### 1. What does "sharing messages and images" mean?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Deleting a photo forever
2. Sending words, pictures, or videos to another person or group, usually through a phone or app
3. Taking a photo with a camera
4. Turning off a phone
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. Sharing messages and images means sending words, pictures, or videos to another person or a group, usually through a phone, app, or website. It can be fun and kind, but it needs some thought first.

    **Concept Tested:** Sharing Messages And Images

#### 2. A student wants to share a funny video of a friend tripping and falling. What is the best thing to do first?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Share it right away since it's funny
2. Send it to as many people as possible
3. Post it without telling anyone
4. Ask whether the friend in the video would be okay with it being shared
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. Before sharing anything, it helps to ask, "Would the person in this picture be okay with me sharing it?" If the answer is no or unsure, it is a sign to stop and rethink.

    **Concept Tested:** Sharing Messages And Images

#### 3. Which piece of personal information should almost never be shared with people a student doesn't know well?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. A home address
2. A favorite color
3. A favorite sport
4. A favorite book
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. A home address is personal information that could let a stranger find or contact a person. It should almost never be shared publicly or with people a student does not know well in real life.

    **Concept Tested:** Risks Of Sharing Personal Information

#### 5. What is "injury prevention"?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Waiting to see if an injury happens before doing anything
2. A game played at recess
3. A rule that only applies at swimming pools
4. Actions people take to reduce the chance of getting hurt
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. Injury prevention means the actions people take to reduce the chance of getting hurt, in places like home, school, playground, road, or water — such as wearing a helmet or using a crosswalk.

    **Concept Tested:** Injury Prevention

---

#### 6. A student is deciding whether riding a bike without a helmet on a quiet sidewalk is "safe enough." Which question helps them evaluate this safety behavior?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. What color is the bike?
2. What could go wrong, and how serious would it be?
3. How fast can the bike go?
4. Is the sidewalk long or short?
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. Evaluating a safety behavior means asking what could go wrong, how likely it is, and how serious the injury could be. Falls happen unpredictably, and head injuries can be serious, even without a helmet rule being broken that particular day.

    **Concept Tested:** Evaluating Safety Behaviors

---

#### 7. What is a "safety procedure"?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Specific steps a school, family, or community follows to stay safe during an emergency
2. A game played during recess
3. A poster hung in the hallway
4. A rule about homework
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. Safety procedures are the specific steps a school, family, or community follows to keep people safe during an emergency, such as a fire drill, tornado drill, or lockdown.

    **Concept Tested:** Safety Procedures

---

#### 8. Why does practicing a fire drill many times help students during a real fire?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. It makes the school day shorter
2. It uses up extra time in the schedule
3. Practiced steps become familiar, so a person can act fast and calm even if they feel scared
4. It is required only once a year
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. Practicing safety procedures means rehearsing the exact steps ahead of time, so during a real emergency, people can respond quickly and calmly instead of feeling confused — even if their mind still feels nervous.

    **Concept Tested:** Practicing Safety Procedures

---

#### 9. What is a "trusted adult"?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Any adult who happens to be nearby
2. Only a student's teacher
3. A grown-up a student can go to for help who will listen and take the concern seriously
4. A grown-up the student has never met
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. A trusted adult is a grown-up a student can go to for help with a difficult or unsafe situation — someone who will listen carefully, take the concern seriously, and help figure out what to do next.

    **Concept Tested:** Trusted Adult

#### 10. Which of these is a good reason to go to a trusted adult?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. A friend picked a different game to play at recess
2. Something feels wrong, even if it's hard to explain why
3. A classmate has a different favorite color
4. It is almost time for lunch
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **B**. A student never needs a perfect explanation to ask for help. "Something feels wrong" is always a good enough reason to go to a trusted adult.

    **Concept Tested:** Trusted Adult
