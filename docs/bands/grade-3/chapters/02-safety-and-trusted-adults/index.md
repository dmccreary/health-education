# Safety And Trusted Adults

## Summary

Students explore how shared messages, images, and personal information can affect themselves and others, how to evaluate behaviors that prevent injuries, and how practicing safety procedures prepares everyone for real emergencies. The chapter also introduces the idea of a trusted adult who can help in difficult or unsafe situations.

## Concepts Covered

1. Sharing Messages And Images
2. Risks Of Sharing Personal Information
3. Injury Prevention
4. Evaluating Safety Behaviors
5. Safety Procedures
6. Practicing Safety Procedures
7. Trusted Adult

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on the foundational ideas about health introduced in [Chapter 1: Foundations Of Health](../01-foundations-of-health/index.md).
