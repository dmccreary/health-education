# Growth And Puberty

## Summary

Students are introduced to puberty and its stages, and learn how to ask a trusted adult for help managing physical and emotional changes during this time of growth.

## Concepts Covered

1. Puberty
2. Stages Of Puberty
3. Asking For Help With Body Changes
4. Identifying Trusted Adults

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on the trusted-adult and safety-procedure concepts from [Chapter 2: Safety And Trusted Adults](../02-safety-and-trusted-adults/index.md).
