# Mental And Emotional Health

## Summary

This chapter explores resilience and how personal choices affect mental and emotional wellbeing, ways to cope with challenging situations and who to talk to, and what mental health challenges are and how they affect people. Students also build empathy and examine how emotions influence and are expressed through behavior.

## Concepts Covered

1. Resilience
2. Personal Choices And Wellbeing
3. Coping With Challenges
4. Who To Talk To
5. Mental Health Challenge
6. Effects Of Mental Health Challenges
7. Empathy
8. Empathy In Friendships
9. Emotions Influence Behavior
10. Expressing Emotions
11. Kindness And Respect For All Abilities

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on the foundational ideas about health in [Chapter 1: Foundations Of Health](../01-foundations-of-health/index.md), the trusted-adult concept from [Chapter 2: Safety And Trusted Adults](../02-safety-and-trusted-adults/index.md), and the relationships and bullying concepts from [Chapter 5: Relationships And Respect](../05-relationships-and-respect/index.md).
