# Relationships And Respect

## Summary

This chapter examines the roles family members and peers play in healthy relationships, the right to personal boundaries, and how teasing, exclusion, or bullying affects individuals and the community. Students learn why treating all people of all abilities with kindness and respect matters.

## Concepts Covered

1. Family Member Roles
2. Peer Roles In Relationships
3. Healthy Relationships
4. Bullying
5. Exclusion
6. Effects Of Bullying And Exclusion
7. Right To Personal Boundaries

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on the foundational ideas about health introduced in [Chapter 1: Foundations Of Health](../01-foundations-of-health/index.md).
