# Communication And Goals

## Summary

Students practice verbal and nonverbal communication, active listening, and using I-statements to express thoughts, feelings, wants, and needs, including in decision-making situations. The chapter closes with setting a personal health goal, judging whether help is needed, and identifying health topics that matter most.

## Concepts Covered

1. Verbal Communication
2. Nonverbal Communication
3. Active Listening
4. Communication In Decision-Making
5. I-Statement
6. Personal Health Goal
7. Determining Need For Assistance
8. Examining Health-Supporting Practices
9. Identifying Relevant Health Topics

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on the foundational ideas about health in [Chapter 1: Foundations Of Health](../01-foundations-of-health/index.md), the coping and who-to-talk-to concepts from [Chapter 7: Mental And Emotional Health](../07-mental-and-emotional-health/index.md), and the physical activity concepts from [Chapter 3: Wellness And Activity](../03-wellness-and-activity/index.md).
