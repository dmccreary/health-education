# Grade 3 Chapters

This section presents the Grade 3 Health Education material as eight chapters, arranged so that each chapter only relies on ideas introduced in earlier chapters. Read the chapters in order the first time through — later chapters (like Growth And Puberty and Communication And Goals) reuse ideas such as trusted adults and coping skills that are introduced earlier. Each chapter page lists its exact learning-graph concepts and links back to the chapter(s) it depends on, so you can jump back to review a prerequisite whenever something feels unfamiliar.

1. [Foundations Of Health](01-foundations-of-health/index.md) — Introduces health as a foundation for the course and the skills for judging whether health information is trustworthy.
2. [Safety And Trusted Adults](02-safety-and-trusted-adults/index.md) — Covers safe sharing of information, injury prevention, safety procedures, and the idea of a trusted adult.
3. [Wellness And Activity](03-wellness-and-activity/index.md) — Explores traditional health knowledge, physical activity, dressing for weather, and germ-prevention practices.
4. [Food And Nutrition](04-food-and-nutrition/index.md) — Covers hunger and fullness signals, nutrition labels, healthy beverage choices, and food safety.
5. [Relationships And Respect](05-relationships-and-respect/index.md) — Examines family and peer roles, personal boundaries, and the effects of bullying and exclusion.
6. [Growth And Puberty](06-growth-and-puberty/index.md) — Introduces puberty, its stages, and asking a trusted adult for help with body changes.
7. [Mental And Emotional Health](07-mental-and-emotional-health/index.md) — Builds resilience, coping skills, empathy, and understanding of mental health challenges.
8. [Communication And Goals](08-communication-and-goals/index.md) — Practices verbal and nonverbal communication, I-statements, and setting a personal health goal.

## How To Use This Textbook

Work through the chapters in numerical order the first time, since each one builds on concepts from the chapters before it. Every chapter page opens with a short summary and a numbered list of the exact concepts it covers, so you can preview what's ahead or search back for a specific term. The "Prerequisites" section at the bottom of each chapter links to the earlier chapter(s) it depends on — use those links to jump back and review before moving forward.
