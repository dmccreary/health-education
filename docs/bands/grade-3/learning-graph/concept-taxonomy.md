# Concept Taxonomy — Grade 3

Given the 57-concept graph for this band, 7 categories are used — the same
scheme as Kindergarten, Grade 1, and Grade 2 — seeded from the five content
strands in the course description plus the cross-cutting Skill Standards
group, with a small Foundations category for the bridging/root concept.
Category names and abbreviations are kept identical to the earlier bands'
schemes, so the taxonomy is comparable across bands.

| Category Name | TaxonomyID | Count | Percentage | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mental and Emotional Health | EMOT | 14 | 24.6% | Resilience, coping with challenges, mental health challenges, empathy, emotion expression, bullying/exclusion, kindness and respect for all abilities |
| Skill Standards | SKIL | 12 | 21.1% | Cross-cutting influences on health behavior, validity/reliability of health information, verbal/nonverbal communication, active listening, I-statements, goal-setting |
| Food and Nutrition | FOOD | 10 | 17.5% | Hunger/fullness signals, carbohydrates/protein/fats, nutrition labels, limiting caffeinated/sugary drinks, beverages for activity, food safety and preparation |
| Personal Safety and Violence Prevention | SAFE | 9 | 15.8% | Sharing messages/information risks, injury prevention, safety procedures, trusted adults, personal boundaries |
| Human Growth and Development | GROW | 6 | 10.5% | Family and peer roles, healthy relationships, puberty and its stages, asking for help with body changes |
| Personal Health and Wellness | WELL | 5 | 8.8% | Traditional health knowledge, benefits of physical activity, dressing for weather, cultural germ-prevention practices |
| Foundations | FOUND | 1 | 1.8% | Root/bridging concept: Health |

No category exceeds the 30% threshold. See
[taxonomy-distribution.md](./taxonomy-distribution.md) for the full generated
distribution report.
