# Kindergarten Chapters

This section contains the read-aloud chapters for the Kindergarten Health Education band. Each chapter groups related concepts from the [learning graph](../learning-graph/learning-graph.json) into a short, teacher-led lesson. Chapters are ordered so that every idea builds only on ideas already covered — start at Chapter 1 and move forward in order, or jump to any chapter your class is ready for since prerequisite links are noted on each chapter page. Content is designed to be read aloud, image-led, and free of multi-step reasoning, matching the pre-reader Kindergarten audience.

1. [Health And Food](01-health-and-food/index.md) — This opening chapter introduces the big idea of health and the foods that help bodies grow strong, including the five food groups, cultural food traditions, hydration, hunger, and food safety.
2. [Family And Trusted Adults](02-family/index.md) — This chapter introduces trusted adults who help keep children healthy and safe, and explores how families come in many different structures.
3. [Feelings And Kindness](03-feelings-and-kindness/index.md) — Students learn to name their own feelings, recognize strong or uncomfortable feelings, ask a trusted adult for help, and practice kindness and inclusion.
4. [Staying Healthy Every Day](04-staying-healthy/index.md) — This chapter covers daily habits that keep bodies healthy, including handwashing, covering coughs, and staying active through daily movement.
5. [Staying Safe](05-staying-safe/index.md) — Students learn safety rules that prevent injuries, healthy technology use, what to do during a school emergency, and the correct names for body parts.
6. [Making Healthy Choices](06-health-choices/index.md) — This closing chapter introduces positive and negative influences on health, recognizing when a health decision is needed, and setting a personal health goal.
