# Personal Safety and Refusal Skills

## Summary

This chapter examines how family, peers, culture, and media/technology influence behavior, including harmful technology use. Students build situational awareness, learn to recognize warning signs and exit unsafe situations, set personal boundaries, and practice refusal skills for resisting peer pressure.

## Concepts Covered

1. Influences On Health Behavior
2. Media And Technology Influence
3. Harmful Technology Behavior
4. Situational Awareness
5. Exiting Unsafe Situations
6. Personal Boundaries
7. Setting Boundaries
8. Warning Sign
9. Peer Pressure
10. Refusal Skill
11. Demonstrating Refusal Skills

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on
[Chapter 3: Mental and Emotional Health](../03-mental-emotional-health/index.md),
which establishes emotional self-regulation skills that support recognizing
and responding to unsafe or pressured situations.
