# Wellness and Disease Prevention

## Summary

This chapter covers common causes of allergic reactions and prevention strategies, and how cultural traditions such as dance and community sports provide physical activity benefits for the body and mood. Students also distinguish infectious from non-infectious disease, learn how germs spread, and study prevention strategies alongside cultural perspectives on wellness and caregiving.

## Concepts Covered

1. Allergy
2. Allergy Prevention And Management
3. Cultural Physical Activity Traditions
4. Benefits Of Physical Activity On Mood
5. Infectious Disease
6. Non-Infectious Disease
7. How Germs Spread
8. Disease Prevention Strategies
9. Cultural Wellness Perspectives

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on
[Chapter 2: Growth and Development Across Cultures](../02-growth-and-development/index.md),

discussion of cultural physical-activity traditions and wellness
perspectives depends on.
