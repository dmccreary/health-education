# Body Safety and Speaking Up

## Summary

This short, focused chapter addresses recognizing unwanted or inappropriate touch and practicing how to say no or exit the situation. It is intentionally kept separate from other topics so it can be taught, and revisited, as a standalone lesson on personal body safety. After completing this chapter, students will be able to identify an unwanted touch and demonstrate saying no or leaving an unsafe situation.

## Concepts Covered

This chapter covers the following 2 concepts from the learning graph:

1. Unwanted Touch
2. Saying No Or Exiting
