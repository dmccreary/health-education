# Staying Healthy in Our Environment

## Summary

This chapter introduces environmental health risks such as sun exposure, loud noise, unsafe chemicals, and unsafe water, and how to protect the body from them. Students learn about the value of physical activity, including cultural and traditional games or dances, and explore how germs spread through air, food, water, and personal contact along with the hygiene practices that prevent illness. After completing this chapter, students will be able to describe an environmental health risk, name a form of physical activity, and explain how illness prevention works.

## Concepts Covered

This chapter covers the following 8 concepts from the learning graph:

1. Environmental Health Risk
2. Sun And Noise Protection
3. Unsafe Chemicals And Water
4. Physical Activity
5. Cultural Physical Activity
6. Germs
7. How Germs Spread
8. Illness Prevention Practice
