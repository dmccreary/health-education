# Chapters

This Grade 2 section of the textbook is organized into 7 chapters covering
55 concepts from the Grade 2 learning graph.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Health Basics, Friendships, and Feelings](01-health-friendships-feelings/index.md) - Introduces the idea of health itself, peer relationships, and early emotional-health tools like coping strategies, personal space, boundaries, and I-statements.
2. [Food, Energy, and Safe Eating](02-food-energy-safe-eating/index.md) - Explores what food groups do for the body, how variety and energy support growth, and why safe storage, preparation, and handling of food matter.
3. [Handling Conflict and Kind Play](03-conflict-kind-play/index.md) - Builds on early emotional-health tools to address conflict resolution, managing strong emotions, playful interaction, and the effects of teasing.
4. [Staying Healthy in Our Environment](04-staying-healthy-environment/index.md) - Covers protecting the body from environmental health risks, the value of physical activity, and how germs spread and can be prevented.
5. [Technology, Community, and Emergency Safety](05-technology-community-emergency-safety/index.md) - Addresses safe technology use, encouraging safe choices through community values, staying safe during drills, and finding health helpers.
6. [Body Safety and Speaking Up](06-body-safety-speaking-up/index.md) - A focused chapter on recognizing unwanted touch and practicing how to say no or exit an unsafe situation.
7. [Influences, Decisions, and Personal Goals](07-influences-decisions-goals/index.md) - Ties the band together with how family, school, and media influence health behaviors, how to weigh decisions, and how to set and reflect on personal health goals.

## How to Use This Textbook

Work through the chapters in order — each one builds on concepts introduced
earlier, so completing chapters 1 through 6 will prepare students for the
decision-making and goal-setting skills in Chapter 7. Teachers may revisit
Chapter 6 (Body Safety and Speaking Up) as a standalone lesson whenever a
sensitive-topics refresher is needed, since it does not depend on chapters
after it.

---

**Note:** Each chapter includes a list of concepts covered. Make sure to
complete prerequisites before moving to advanced chapters.
