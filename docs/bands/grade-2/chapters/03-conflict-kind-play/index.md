# Handling Conflict and Kind Play

## Summary

Building on the coping strategies and emotional-health foundations from Chapter 1, this chapter addresses healthy ways to handle conflict and manage or express strong emotions, including sharing feelings with a trusted adult. Students also examine playful interaction and how different types of teasing affect feelings and community. After completing this chapter, students will be able to describe a healthy conflict-resolution step and explain how teasing can affect others.

## Concepts Covered

This chapter covers the following 8 concepts from the learning graph:

1. Conflict
2. Healthy Conflict Resolution
3. Managing Strong Emotions
4. Sharing Feelings With Trusted Adult
5. Playful Interaction
6. Teasing
7. Effects Of Teasing
8. Recognizing Feelings About Behaviors
