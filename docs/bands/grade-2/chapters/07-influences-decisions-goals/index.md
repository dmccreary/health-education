# Influences, Decisions, and Personal Goals

## Summary

This capstone chapter examines how family, school, and media or technology influence health practices and behaviors, and revisits active listening as a communication skill. Students learn to describe options and potential outcomes for a health-related decision, then apply goal-setting by determining actions that support a personal health goal and reflecting on their progress. The chapter closes with modeling positive health choices for others. After completing this chapter, students will be able to identify an influence on their health behavior, weigh a decision's outcomes, and set and reflect on a personal health goal.
