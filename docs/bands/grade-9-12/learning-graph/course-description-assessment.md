# Course Description Quality Assessment — Grade 9-12

## Source Document

[docs/bands/grade-9-12/course-description.md](../course-description.md)

## Summary

The Grade 9-12 course description is the largest and most cognitively demanding
document in the series. It is derived from the 2025 Minnesota K-12 Health
Academic Standards and enumerates **61 benchmark-coded topics** across six
content strands (Food and Nutrition; Human Growth, Development, and Sexual
Health; Mental and Emotional Health; Personal Health and Wellness; Personal
Safety and Violence Prevention; Substance Use Awareness and Prevention) plus a
large cross-cutting Skill Standards group (20 benchmarks). This slightly
exceeds Grade 6-8's 59 topic benchmarks + 19 Skill Standards benchmarks
(78 total), and is the terminal band, culminating in career/college/community
readiness framing.

This assessment evaluates the description against a right-sized concept
target of approximately 130-145 concepts (not the skill's generic 200-concept
default), consistent with the proportional scaling applied to Grade 6-8
(120-135 concepts) and reflecting a modest increase in raw benchmark count
plus this band's markedly higher density of compound, multi-clause benchmarks.

## Strengths

- Every topic traces to a specific MDE benchmark code, giving the learning
  graph strong provenance — 61 topic benchmarks plus 20 Skill Standards
  benchmarks (81 total), the largest of any band in this series.

- Explicitly documents this band's distinguishing cognitive signature —
  heavy repeated use of "evaluate" verbs — and the Evaluate tier below
  delivers on that claim with 29 traced outcomes, roughly triple any other
  band's Evaluate tier.

- Strong "Bridging Concepts for Students New to This Band" section
  separates content carried forward from Grade 6-8 (nutrition labels,
  relationship structures and consent, mental-health warning signs, fitness
  components, CPR/AED, digital-safety boundaries, substance-use
  consequences, structured decision-making) from genuinely new emphases at
  this band (systemic/cultural analysis, cultural safety in healthcare,
  tribal sovereignty, Indigenous restorative justice).

- Consistently threads cultural responsiveness and Indigenous/tribal
  perspectives through nearly every strand (culturally safe healthcare,
  Indigenous views of health, traditional plant medicine, tribal
  sovereignty, restorative justice), giving the band's concepts a distinct,
  non-redundant character relative to Grade 6-8.

- Statewide-mandated topics (CPR/AED, vaping, cannabis/fentanyl, STI/HIV/HPV,
  suicide/self-harm) are explicitly flagged as culminating in this band,
  strengthening traceability to the primary standards document.

## Gaps

- No dedicated "Topics Excluded" section (partial credit given based on
  related context in the Reading Level & Cognitive Demand section).

- Remember-level outcomes are limited to 2, the thinnest tier in the
  document — but this is an accurate reflection of the standards' own
  emphasis on Evaluate-level cognitive demand at the terminal band, not a
  document defect.

- Apply-level outcomes (6) are comparatively modest relative to
  Analyze/Evaluate, though several Analyze/Evaluate outcomes at this band
  implicitly require application as a precondition.

## Concept Count Guidance

Per explicit run-level override, this band targets approximately 130-145
concepts (not 200), built from the 61 enumerated topic benchmarks, the 20
Skill Standards benchmarks, the 20 Key Vocabulary terms, and a small number
of clearly-implied bridging/connective concepts carried forward from Grade
6-8. This is a modest increase over Grade 6-8's 133-concept graph,
proportional to the slight increase in benchmark count (78 to 81 total
benchmarks) and this band's higher density of compound, multi-clause
benchmarks that split naturally into 2-3 sub-concepts each.
