# Healthcare Access and Sexual Health

## Summary

This chapter examines how trusted adults and culturally safe, youth-friendly
healthcare providers support adolescents' rights to access care, including
STI prevention and treatment. Students evaluate how stigma affects
healthcare access and how community-based programs and personal boundaries
work together to prevent pregnancy and STIs.

## Concepts Covered

1. Trusted Adult Guidance
2. Culturally Safe Healthcare Providers
3. Adolescent Healthcare Access Rights
4. Youth-Friendly Provider Expectations
5. Stigma Effects On Healthcare Access
6. STI Prevention And Treatment
7. Culturally Safe STI Care
8. Healthcare Services Supporting Sexual Health
9. Community-Based Sexual Health Programs
10. Preventing Pregnancy And STIs Through Boundaries

## Prerequisites

Builds on personal boundary communication and consent from
[Chapter 3: Consent and Boundaries](../03-consent-and-boundaries/index.md).
