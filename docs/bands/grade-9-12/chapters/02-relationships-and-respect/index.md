# Relationships and Respect

## Summary

This chapter examines how relationship structures form and vary across
people and cultures, what characterizes a healthy relationship, and how to
handle conflict and pressure within one. Students analyze how cultural and
historical context, stereotypes, and bias shape relationships, and practice
empathy as a foundation for healthy connection.

## Concepts Covered

1. Relationship Structures
2. Healthy Relationship Characteristics
3. Handling Relationship Conflict And Pressure
4. Cultural And Historical Influences On Relationships
5. Stereotypes And Bias Effects On Relationships
6. Empathy In Relationships

## Prerequisites

Builds on the concept of health introduced in
