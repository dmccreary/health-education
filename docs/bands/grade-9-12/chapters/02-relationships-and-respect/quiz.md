#### 1. What is a "relationship structure"?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. The particular form a connection between people takes, including who is involved and what roles they hold
2. A legal contract that only applies to marriage
3. A fixed, universal pattern that all healthy relationships must follow
4. A ranking of which types of relationships matter most
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. A relationship structure is the particular form a connection takes: who is involved, what roles they hold, what commitments bind them, and what the surrounding community recognizes. Friendships, chosen family, blended families, and romantic partnerships of many kinds are all structures, and no single one is the default that others deviate from.

#### 2. Which of the following is one of the six healthy relationship characteristics described in the chapter?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>

2. Limiting a partner's outside friendships to protect the relationship
3. Avoiding disagreement entirely to keep the peace
4. Equitable power, where decisions are made together
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **D**. #### 6. A partner asks once whether their significant other wants to attend a family event. The significant other says no, and the partner drops it without further comment. What does this scenario represent?

    The correct answer is **D**. A single request is not pressure, and respecting the answer once it's given is the healthy pattern. The chapter's decision guide specifically identifies repetition *after* a clearly stated boundary, not the initial request itself, as the warning sign that separates ordinary conflict from coercive pressure.

#### 9. In the chapter's conflict-or-pressure decision guide, a request is repeated after a boundary has already been clearly stated, and it now includes guilt-tripping language. According to the decision path, what does this combination indicate?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>

</div>

    The correct answer is **A**. Persistence without those tactics is treated differently, as non-coercive but still requiring a direct response.
