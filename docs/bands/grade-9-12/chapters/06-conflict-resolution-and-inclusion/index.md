# Conflict Resolution and Inclusion

## Summary

This chapter develops communication skills for nonviolent conflict
resolution, including Indigenous restorative-justice approaches, and
strategies for managing emotions using cultural teachings. Students then
design strategies for building supportive, inclusive environments that
actively address exclusion, bullying, stereotyping, and discrimination.

## Concepts Covered

1. Communication For Conflict Resolution
2. Nonviolent Conflict Resolution
3. Indigenous Restorative Justice Approaches
4. Emotion Management Strategies
5. Cultural Teachings For Wellbeing
6. Inclusive Environment Design
7. Addressing Exclusion And Bullying
8. Addressing Stereotyping And Discrimination

## Prerequisites

Builds on handling relationship conflict from
[Chapter 2: Relationships and Respect](../02-relationships-and-respect/index.md)
and dignity and compassion from
[Chapter 3: Consent and Boundaries](../03-consent-and-boundaries/index.md).
