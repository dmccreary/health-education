# Digital and School Safety

## Summary

This chapter evaluates how digital content and social media affect
relationships, expectations, and boundaries, and covers safe, appropriate
actions for online threats, harmful content, and emergencies. Students also
evaluate school safety practices and policies, including preparedness for
school shootings.

## Concepts Covered

1. Digital Content Effects On Relationships
2. Social Media Effects On Boundaries
3. Responding To Online Threats And Harmful Content
4. Online Emergency Response
5. School Safety Practices Evaluation
6. School Shooting Preparedness

## Prerequisites

Builds on healthy-relationship characteristics from
[Chapter 2: Relationships and Respect](../02-relationships-and-respect/index.md),
personal boundary communication from
[Chapter 3: Consent and Boundaries](../03-consent-and-boundaries/index.md),
and trusted adult guidance from
[Chapter 4: Healthcare Access and Sexual Health](../04-healthcare-access-and-sexual-health/index.md).
