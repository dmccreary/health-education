    The correct answer is **A**. #### 7. A school responds to a new respiratory virus outbreak by improving classroom ventilation only, without addressing vaccination rates. According to the chapter's transmission chain reasoning, what is the likely limitation of this approach?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Ventilation targets person-to-person spread, but exposed unvaccinated individuals can still become ill since the new host's immune defense point remains unaddressed
2. Ventilation has no effect on respiratory virus transmission at all
3. This approach fully eliminates transmission risk since ventilation addresses every point in the chain
4. Vaccination and ventilation address the exact same transmission point, making one of them redundant
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **A**. The chapter explains that improving ventilation targets the person-to-person spread point in the transmission chain, but vaccination targets a separate point: the new host's immune defense. Using only ventilation still allows illness in unvaccinated individuals who are exposed, since good airflow alone doesn't prepare anyone's immune system in advance.

    **Concept Tested:** Infectious Disease Prevention Strategies
