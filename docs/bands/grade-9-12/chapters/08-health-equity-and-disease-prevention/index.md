# Health Equity and Disease Prevention

## Summary

This chapter analyzes how social conditions create health disparities and
systemic inequity in access to physical activity, and how to participate
safely despite those barriers. Students then evaluate behavioral and
environmental factors that drive chronic and infectious disease, and
strategies to prevent them.

## Concepts Covered

1. Health Disparities From Social Conditions
2. Physical Activity Access Across Communities
3. Safe Physical Activity Participation
4. Systemic Inequity In Health Opportunity
5. Behavioral And Environmental Factors In Disease Risk
6. Chronic Disease Prevention Strategies
7. Infectious Disease Prevention Strategies

## Prerequisites

Builds on food systems and chronic-disease risk from
[Chapter 1: Food and Nutrition](../01-food-and-nutrition/index.md)
and stigma effects on healthcare access from
[Chapter 4: Healthcare Access and Sexual Health](../04-healthcare-access-and-sexual-health/index.md).
