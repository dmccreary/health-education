# Substance Policy and Law

## Summary

This chapter evaluates community laws, policies, and the financial and
social costs of substance use, including tribal sovereignty in lawmaking.
Students analyze how marketing and availability policy shapes substance use
and the health disparities that result.

## Concepts Covered

1. Community Laws On Substance Use
2. Financial And Social Costs Of Substance Use
3. Tribal Sovereignty In Substance Use Lawmaking
4. Substance Marketing And Availability Policy
5. Substance-Related Health Disparities

## Prerequisites

Builds on substance use effects on relationships, school, and work from
[Chapter 10: Substances: Effects and Safer Choices](../10-substances-effects-and-safer-choices/index.md)
and health disparities from
[Chapter 8: Health Equity and Disease Prevention](../08-health-equity-and-disease-prevention/index.md).
