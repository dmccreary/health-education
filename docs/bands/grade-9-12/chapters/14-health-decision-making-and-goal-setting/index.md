# Health Decision-Making and Goal-Setting

## Summary

This chapter applies a structured decision-making process to weigh how
health decisions affect personal and community wellbeing from multiple
perspectives, evaluate options and consequences, and choose and justify the
healthiest option. Students then use that same structured approach to
evaluate focus areas and set personal health goals individually, with
support, or collaboratively.

## Concepts Covered

1. Health Decisions And Personal Wellbeing
2. Health Decisions And Community Wellbeing
3. Multiple Perspectives On Health Decisions
4. Evaluating Options In Health Decisions
5. Evaluating Consequences In Health Decisions
6. Choosing The Healthiest Option
7. Justifying Health Choices
8. Evaluating Health Decision Outcomes
9. Health Goal Focus Area Evaluation
10. Individual Goal-Setting Process
11. Supported Goal-Setting Process
12. Collaborative Goal-Setting Process
13. Formulating Goal Strategies
14. Evaluating Goal Strategy Outcomes

## Prerequisites

Builds on individual and interpersonal factors in health practices and
community/societal/environmental factors from
[Chapter 13: Influences on Health Behavior](../13-influences-on-health-behavior/index.md).
