# Food And Nutrition

## Summary

Students compare how different cultures meet daily nutrient and energy needs, then learn to read nutrition labels to choose healthier foods and drinks. The chapter builds toward planning nutritious meals and snacks and understanding how unsafe food handling causes foodborne illness.

## Concepts Covered

1. Cultural Nutrient Approaches
2. Daily Energy Needs
3. Nutrition Label Comparison
4. Choosing The Healthier Food
5. Water As The Healthy Drink Choice
6. Comparing Water To Other Drinks
7. Nutritious Meal Planning
8. Nutritious Snack Choices
9. Foodborne Illness
10. Unsafe Food Handling Practices

## Prerequisites

Builds on the concept of Health introduced in [Chapter 1: Foundations And Trusted Adults](../01-foundations-and-trusted-adults/index.md).
