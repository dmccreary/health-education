# Foundations And Trusted Adults

## Summary

This opening chapter grounds the Grade 5 band in the idea of health itself and in the people and relationships that shape a student's choices. Students examine how family and peer relationships influence feelings, behavior, and decisions, and how emotions in turn shape behavior. The chapter closes by building the skill of identifying a trusted adult and telling that adult about a health concern, a skill referenced throughout every later chapter.

## Concepts Covered

1. Health
2. Family Influence On Decisions
3. Peer Influence On Decisions
4. Emotions Shape Behavior
5. Trusted Adult
6. Telling A Trusted Adult
7. Health Concern Reporting

## Prerequisites

This is the first chapter in the Grade 5 band. It assumes only the Grade 4 prerequisites and bridging concepts described in the [course description](../../course-description.md).
