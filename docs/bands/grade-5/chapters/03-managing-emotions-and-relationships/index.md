# Managing Emotions And Relationships

## Summary

Students learn restorative strategies for managing their own personal growth and their relationships with others, including how to support a peer in a difficult situation while respecting boundaries and guiding them toward help. The chapter also examines the situations and experiences that trigger emotions and how those emotions influence behavior.

## Concepts Covered

1. Restorative Self-Management Strategies
2. Managing Personal Growth
3. Managing Relationships
4. Supporting A Peer In Difficulty
5. Respecting Peer Boundaries
6. Guiding A Peer To Help
7. Emotional Triggers
8. Situations That Influence Emotions

## Prerequisites

Builds on Emotions Shape Behavior and Family/Peer Influence On Decisions from [Chapter 1: Foundations And Trusted Adults](../01-foundations-and-trusted-adults/index.md).
