Test your understanding of social and cultural influences on health, evaluating health information, and communication skills with these review questions.

#### 1. According to the chapter, what does "congruence" mean in communication?

<div class="upper-alpha" markdown>
1. Speaking loudly enough for everyone in the room to hear
2. Using only nonverbal signals without any words at all
3. Verbal and nonverbal signals matching each other, such as saying "I'm fine" in a calm tone with relaxed posture
4. Repeating the same sentence multiple times during a conversation
</div>

??? question "Show Answer"
    The correct answer is **C**. Congruence is when verbal and nonverbal signals match, such as saying "I'm fine" in a calm tone with relaxed posture. Incongruence, when the two conflict, is usually a signal that something is being left unsaid.

    **Concept Tested:** Verbal And Nonverbal Communication
