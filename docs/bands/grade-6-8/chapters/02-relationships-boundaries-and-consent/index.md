# Relationships, Boundaries, And Consent

## Summary

This chapter examines the structures that relationships can take and what distinguishes healthy from unhealthy dynamics across family, peer, and romantic contexts. Students learn to set personal boundaries informed by cultural values of respect and balance, and to clearly distinguish consent from coercion. The chapter extends these ideas into how to recognize and respond to coercion in real situations.

## Concepts Covered

1. Relationship Structures
2. Healthy Relationship Characteristics
3. Unhealthy Relationship Characteristics
4. Romantic Relationships
5. Personal Boundary-Setting
6. Cultural Values Of Respect And Balance
7. Consent
8. Coercion
9. Consent Versus Coercion
10. Consent In Healthy Relationships
11. Responding To Coercion

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on concepts introduced in [Health Foundations And Nutrition](../01-health-foundations-and-nutrition/index.md).
