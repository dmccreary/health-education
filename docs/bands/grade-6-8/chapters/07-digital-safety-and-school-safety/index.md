# Digital Safety And School Safety

## Summary

This chapter examines how sending, sharing, or viewing harmful or explicit content affects behavior, relationships, and values, including legal and social consequences. Students analyze power dynamics, peer pressure, and digital communication, and practice boundary-setting and seeking support. The chapter concludes with school safety: recognizing threats, reporting concerns, and practicing emergency responses.

## Concepts Covered

1. Harmful Or Explicit Content Sharing
2. Consequences Of Sharing Explicit Content
3. Power Dynamics In Relationships
4. Peer Pressure
5. Digital Communication Effects On Relationships
6. Boundary-Setting Strategies
7. Seeking Support For Relationship Safety
8. School Safety Actions
9. Recognizing Threats
10. Reporting Safety Concerns
11. Practicing Emergency Responses
