# Substance Use Culture, Safety, And Risk

## Summary

This chapter contrasts commercial tobacco addiction with traditional tobacco and plant medicine practices across cultures, including Indigenous peoples. Students learn to read, store, and dispose of medications safely, understand the benefits of being substance-free, and analyze how substance use raises the risk of injury, violence, self-harm, and harm to sexual health.

## Concepts Covered

1. Commercial Tobacco Addiction
2. Traditional Tobacco And Plant Medicine Use
3. Medication Storage And Disposal Safety
4. Reading Medication Labels
5. Traditional Plant Medicine Practices
6. Benefits Of Being Substance-Free
7. Healthy Stress Management Without Substances
8. Substance Use And Injury Risk
9. Substance Use And Violence Risk
10. Substance Use And Self-Harm Risk
11. Substance Use And Sexual Health Risk

## Prerequisites

This chapter builds on concepts introduced in [Healthcare Access And Sexual Health](../03-healthcare-access-and-sexual-health/index.md), [Emotional Well-Being And Mental Health](../04-emotional-well-being-and-mental-health/index.md), [Substances And The Developing Brain](../08-substances-and-the-developing-brain/index.md).
