| **2025 Minnesota K-12 Health Academic Standards — Commissioner Approved (12/15/25)** | Minnesota Department of Education (MDE) | **This is the source used to design this course's age/grade tiers.** The proposed standards document, approved by the Commissioner in December 2025 and reviewed at an administrative rulemaking hearing (April 13-14, 2026; public comment closed 2026-05-04). Per the MDE site, locally developed standards remain in effect until formal statewide adoption completes — this document is not yet the legally operative standard, but represents the finalized proposed content. Structure verified: 8 anchor standards (1 knowledge standard + 7 skill standards — analyzing influences, accessing information, communication, decision-making, goal setting, healthy practices, promoting wellbeing), organized into 6 strands (Food & Nutrition K-12; Human Growth & Development K-5 / + Sexual Health 6-12; Mental and Emotional Health K-12; Personal Health and Wellness K-12; Personal Safety and Violence Prevention K-12; Substance Use Awareness and Prevention 6-12). Benchmarks are tabulated individually for grades K, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, then as grade bands 6-8 and 9-12, with each benchmark coded (e.g. `0.1.1.1` for kindergarten, `9.1.1.1` for grades 9-12). | [PDF](https://education.mn.gov/mdeprod/idcplg?IdcService=GET_FILE&dDocName=PROD098432&RevisionSelectionMethod=latestReleased&Rendition=primary) |
