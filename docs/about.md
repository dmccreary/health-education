# About

Interactive intelligent textbook for health education for K-12 students with age appropriate tiers

## Minnesota's Statewide Health Education Goal

Minnesota is working to replace a patchwork of locally developed health
standards with its first consistent statewide K-12 framework. The key goals
are:

- **Equity:** Give students comparable health education regardless of their
  district or ZIP code.
- **Clear progression:** Define age-appropriate benchmarks for grades K-5 and
  the grade bands 6-8 and 9-12.
- **Skills over memorization:** Help students practice evaluating information,
  communicating, making decisions, setting goals, adopting healthy behaviors,
  and promoting health.
- **Comprehensive coverage:** Address nutrition, human development and sexual
  health, mental and emotional health, personal wellness, safety and violence
  prevention, and substance-use prevention. State-required subjects include
  CPR, vaping, cannabis, sexually transmitted infection prevention, and mental
  health.
- **Continued local curriculum control:** Establish what students should know
  and be able to do while allowing districts to choose curricula, teaching
  methods, and local assessments.
- **Accountability and graduation alignment:** Once implemented, require
  districts to teach and locally assess the standards and require high-school
  credit sufficient to satisfy them.

As of July 6, 2026, the standards have completed drafting, public comment, and
an April administrative hearing, but final adoption and classroom
implementation dates remain to be determined. Locally developed health
standards continue to apply until the statewide standards are implemented.

## How to Use This Book

- Read chapters in order — concepts are introduced in dependency order.
- Use the search bar (top right) to jump to a specific term.
- Try the MicroSims as you encounter them; they are the fastest way to build
  intuition for a new concept.
- Check the [Learning Graph](learning-graph/index.md) when you want to see how
  a concept fits into the larger picture.
