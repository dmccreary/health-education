# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

*Health Education* is a K-12 teaching-resource collection spanning six health
strands — Food and Nutrition, Human Growth and Development, Mental and
Emotional Health, Personal Health and Wellness, Personal Safety and Violence
Prevention, and Substance Use Awareness and Prevention — written for every
grade band from Kindergarten through high school. The intended audience is
K-12 classroom teachers, curriculum coordinators, and students. The visual
tone should be warm, bright, and approachable — cheerful and welcoming like a
friendly classroom poster, not clinical or textbook-dry — while still reading
as credible and well organized.

## Title

Place **Health Education** in the center of the image, in a clean, highly
legible rounded sans-serif font. Use a white (or very light) font color with a
subtle drop shadow or soft dark scrim behind it so it stays readable against
the busy montage background. Keep the two words large and centered; stack them
on two lines ("Health" over "Education") if that reads more clearly. Do not
shrink the title to fit — instead keep generous, calmer negative space directly
behind the text.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each rendered
in a consistent flat-cartoon-vector illustration style (see Style below) so the
composition reads as one cohesive image rather than a collage of unrelated
styles. Spread them in a loose ring around the centered title, and keep the
lower-left corner clear for the mascot:

- **Balanced Plate (Food & Nutrition)** — a colorful top-down dinner plate divided into four food groups: red apple slices and leafy greens, orange carrots, tan whole grains, and a lean protein. Bright and instantly readable.
- **Feelings & Emotions (Mental & Emotional Health)** — a small cluster of three rounded, expressive faces showing happy, calm, and worried feelings, paired with a simple "feelings weather" motif (a sun peeking out from behind a cloud).
- **Handwashing & Hygiene (Personal Health & Wellness)** — a pair of cupped hands covered in white soap bubbles with a small 20-second timer arc curving around them. Clean blue-and-white palette.
- **Active Play (Personal Health & Wellness)** — a cheerful child mid-jump with a jump rope (or kicking a ball) on green grass, conveying movement and physical activity.
- **Bike & Gear Safety (Personal Safety)** — a friendly bicycle helmet with a small buckled seatbelt icon beside it, signaling "buckle up and gear up." A clean, recognizable safety symbol.
- **Safe Voice & Trusted Adults (Personal Safety & Violence Prevention)** — a small child's hand reaching up to a caring grown-up's hand, with a soft speech bubble containing a heart. Rendered sincerely and warmly.
- **The Growing Brain (Substance Use Awareness & Brain Health)** — a friendly, softly glowing stylized brain in teal and purple, suggesting healthy choices and a developing mind.
- **Growing Up (Human Growth & Development)** — a height/growth ruler with three ascending figures (small to taller) beside it, showing growth over time.

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, **Scout the Dog**, in the **lower-left corner** in a
cheerful **welcome / waving pose**, sized so it does not overlap the title text
or crowd the montage elements. Scout is a friendly golden retriever rendered in
flat cartoon vector style: warm golden-tan fur (hex #D9A455), floppy ears, a
simple triangular neckerchief in deep orange (hex #EF6C00), a warm closed-mouth
smile, and soft, kind eyes. In this pose Scout faces the viewer and waves
cheerfully with one front paw raised, expression warm and welcoming — as if
saying "welcome, let's get started." A reference image is available at
`docs/img/mascot/welcome.png`.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: flat cartoon vector with clean lines and gentle rounded shapes — the same style as the Scout mascot — applied consistently to every montage element so the whole cover feels unified and child-friendly.
- Color palette: bright and friendly, anchored by the mascot's golden-tan (#D9A455) and deep orange (#EF6C00), plus fresh leaf green, sky blue, and soft accents of teal and coral. Keep it cheerful and high-clarity, not neon.
- Lighting/mood: bright, optimistic, and welcoming, with soft even lighting and no harsh shadows.
- Composition: title centered with calm negative space behind it; the 8 montage elements arranged in a loose ring around the title; Scout the Dog waving in the lower-left corner. Balance the visual weight so the lower-left mascot is offset by montage elements toward the upper-right.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image (short labels or a single food/emotion icon are fine).
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, people pointing at whiteboards) unless explicitly listed above.
- Avoid photorealistic human faces or realistic medical/clinical imagery — keep people simple, friendly, and cartoon-styled.
- Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the centered title or the lower-left mascot.
- Keep the Personal Safety and Substance Use imagery sincere and reassuring — no scary, graphic, or alarming depictions.
