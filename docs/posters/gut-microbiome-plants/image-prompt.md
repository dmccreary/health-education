## What to Draw

The illustration depicts a human torso silhouette with a simplified, clear anatomical view of the digestive tract, focusing on the large intestine (colon). A large circular magnifying lens or zoom-in bubble overlays the center-right of the colon, showing the microscopic world of the microbiome in detail.

### 1. Large Intestine (Colon)
**Position**: Lower abdominal area of the torso silhouette, centered.
**Visual**: The large intestine is rendered in a soft, light red or pink tone, showing its haustra (segmented pouches). It has a clear, smooth outer boundary.

### 2. Magnifying Cutout (Diverse Microbiome)
**Position**: Circular cutout (approx. 35% of the image width) centered on the middle of the colon.
**Visual**: A crisp, circular lens border (thin light gray or gold glow) enclosing a highly detailed, colorful, and diverse microscopic ecosystem. It shows:
- A rich variety of beneficial bacterial shapes: rod-shaped (Lactobacillus in violet), comma-shaped (Vibrio in teal), spiral helices (Spirillum in gold), and chains of small spheres (Streptococcus in pink).
- Strands of partially digested food fibers (prebiotics) showing varying textures.

### 3. Plant Fibers (Prebiotics)
**Position**: Entering the upper colon from the small intestine (top-center) and mixing into the microbiome cutout.
**Visual**: Stylized organic green and yellow long fiber strands or mesh-like structures that are being surrounded by the bacteria.

### 4. Short-Chain Fatty Acids (SCFAs)
**Position**: Radiating from the bacteria inside the magnifying cutout and crossing the colon wall.
**Visual**: Small, glowing, semi-transparent blue and gold spheres or simple chemical structures representing acetate, propionate, and butyrate.

### 5. Torso and Plant Inputs
**Position**: Torso silhouette is faint light gray in the background; a gentle cascade of clean plant icons (a slice of orange, a broccoli floret, a seed, a grain husk, a almond nut, a chili pepper) descends from the top right toward the digestive system.
**Visual**: Simplified, colored flat icons, stylized and clean, representing the diversity of 30+ plants per week.
