# Where Is My Safe Place? — Image Generation Prompt

Please generate a new image.

## Image specifications

- **Format:** PNG
- **Dimensions:** 1536 × 1024 px, landscape, 3:2 ratio
- **Audience:** Kindergarten students, ages 5–6
- **Style:** Ultra-high-quality modern children's educational illustration; sophisticated editorial infographic; softly dimensional digital gouache; crisp silhouettes; rich harmonious colors; gentle expressions; polished spacing; culturally inclusive characters

## Composition

Create a warm, reassuring poster showing four familiar settings where children follow safety rules and know where to find a trusted adult or planned safe place.

A title band occupies the top 12% of the canvas. Below it are four equal-width vertical illustrated panels with generous gutters and strong visual separation:

- Home: approximately 1–24% of the canvas width
- School: approximately 25–49% of the canvas width
- Playground: approximately 50–74% of the canvas width
- Community: approximately 75–99% of the canvas width
- Panel content: approximately 13–98% of the canvas height

## Exact text

Render **“WHERE IS MY SAFE PLACE?”** verbatim in large, bold, exceptionally legible dark-navy uppercase type.

Render only these four panel headings below it:

- **“HOME”**
- **“SCHOOL”**
- **“PLAYGROUND”**
- **“COMMUNITY”**

No other words, letters, or numbers may appear anywhere.

## Panel artwork

### Home

Use a warm coral and golden palette. Show a cozy home entrance and front yard. A young child and diverse trusted adult stand together at a calm family meeting place outside the doorway. Include a visible stair handrail and safely stored household items, but no danger or emergency.

### School

Use a calm blue palette. Show a teacher leading a short line of children to a sunny outdoor class gathering place beside the school. The children stay together and listen. Use a simple colored ground circle or tree as a visual landmark, with no written signs.

### Playground

Use a bright teal and green palette. Show an inclusive playground with ramps and varied play equipment. A child checks in with a visible playground supervisor or trusted adult near a shaded bench. Other children use equipment safely and take turns.

### Community

Use a reassuring violet and amber palette. Show a neighborhood crosswalk with a crossing guard and a child holding a trusted adult's hand while waiting safely at the curb. Include a community building and a walk signal represented only by a simple human figure icon.

## Critical constraints

- Text must be perfectly spelled and rendered exactly as requested.
- Each panel must remain a large rectangular region suitable for an invisible interactive hover zone.
- Preserve clear gutters and align the four headings at the same height.
- Keep faces, helpers, and safety actions inside their panel boundaries.
- Do not add captions, labels, speech bubbles, UI words, room or street signs with words, book text, logos, branding, signatures, or watermarks.
- Do not add numbers, arrows, callout lines, markers, warning symbols, flames, smoke, storms, injuries, crying, fear, police scenes, danger, or ominous lighting.
- Do not imply one universal emergency plan. Show familiar safe actions and trusted adults.
- The emotional tone is calm competence: grown-ups have plans, and children listen and stay together.
