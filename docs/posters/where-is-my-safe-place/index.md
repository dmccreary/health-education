Use **Explore** mode to select a setting and learn about its safety rules and helpers. Use **Quiz Me** to practice finding the setting described in each question.

The four settings are:

1. **Home** — stay with a trusted adult and know the family plan.
2. **School** — stay with the teacher and class at the practiced safe place.
3. **Playground** — know which supervising adult can help.
4. **Community** — stay with a trusted adult and follow community helpers.

Teachers and editors can open [calibration mode](./main.html?edit=true) to adjust the rectangular zone boundaries and copy the updated JSON.
