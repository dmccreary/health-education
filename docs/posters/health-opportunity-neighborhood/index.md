1. **Food Access** — Distance, price, quality, hours, and transportation shape food access.
2. **Recreation Space** — Safe, welcoming places make movement and connection easier.
3. **Transportation** — Reliable transit connects people with school, work, food, care, and community.
4. **Healthcare** — Nearby care is useful only when it is affordable, respectful, accessible, and available.
5. **Air Quality** — Traffic and industry can concentrate air pollution in some neighborhoods.
6. **Traffic Safety** — Road design affects walking, wheeling, cycling, and injury risk.
7. **Housing Quality** — Stable, safe housing supports sleep, study, stress regulation, and health.
8. **Affordability** — Costs for housing, food, care, and transportation compete within a budget.
9. **Disability Access** — Ramps, curb cuts, surfaces, signage, and inclusive design affect participation.
10. **Schools** — Schools can connect students with learning, meals, activity, care, and belonging.
11. **Work and Time** — Work schedules and travel time affect sleep, meals, caregiving, and healthcare.
12. **Community Garden** — Gardens can support food, knowledge, culture, nature, and connection.
13. **Green Space** — Trees and nature can support cooling, cleaner air, movement, and restoration.
14. **Community Organization** — Residents hold knowledge and organize solutions that fit local needs.
15. **Civic Decisions** — Budgets, zoning, transportation, and environmental rules shape health opportunity.
