1. **Growing Has Many Early Signs** — Growth happens throughout childhood, and outgrown clothes or shoes can be ordinary signs.
2. **Some Changes Begin Earlier** — Some students notice growth changes earlier than classmates.
3. **Some Changes Begin Later** — Other students notice changes later, and that is normal too.
4. **Hygiene Needs May Change** — Growing bodies may sweat more or need adjusted skin and hygiene routines.
5. **Feelings Can Feel Stronger** — Puberty can bring stronger, mixed, or more sudden emotions.
6. **Peers Grow at Different Paces** — Bodies can be at different stages while students share the same interests and belonging.
7. **Ask at Home** — Parents, guardians, and trusted family members can answer questions and listen.
8. **Ask at School** — School nurses, teachers, and counselors can help with questions or feelings during the school day.
9. **Ask a Health Professional** — Doctors and other health professionals can explain body changes and check concerns.
10. **Every Path Can Be Normal** — Puberty unfolds gradually and at different times for different people.
