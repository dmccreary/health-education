# From Bias to Belonging — Image Generation Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: wide interactive educational poster base for Grade 4
Primary request: Create a powerful four-stage visual journey from unfair assumptions to a genuinely welcoming community.
Scene/backdrop: One continuous school arts-and-learning hall divided into four equal scenes, with the same diverse group progressing through reflection and action.
Required text (verbatim, headings only): "RECOGNIZE BIAS", "QUESTION PREJUDICE", "INTERRUPT HARM", "CREATE BELONGING"
Subject details: RECOGNIZE BIAS shows an assumption thought-bubble being noticed and examined with a magnifying glass; QUESTION PREJUDICE shows students comparing an unfair fixed idea with real evidence and individual stories; INTERRUPT HARM shows a calm bystander stopping teasing or exclusion and getting adult support; CREATE BELONGING shows students across cultures, languages, abilities, and family backgrounds co-designing an accessible mural and shared activity.
Style/medium: award-quality children's editorial illustration, sophisticated collage and gouache, expressive but respectful, optimistic without becoming sentimental.
Composition/framing: 1536×1024 wide landscape, four equal vertical panels, heading centered at top of each panel, continuous floor line.
Color palette: cool blue, amber, coral, and vibrant green.
Text constraints: Render exactly the four quoted headings, spelled correctly, and no other words anywhere.
Avoid: stereotypes, caricatures, savior framing, tokenism, red wrong-answer marks, numbers, arrows, callout marks, logos, watermarks.
