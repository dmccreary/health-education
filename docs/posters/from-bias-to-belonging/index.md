1. **Recognize Bias** — Notice when a judgment rests on an assumption instead of real evidence.
2. **Question Prejudice** — Challenge fixed negative opinions by seeking facts and knowing people as individuals.
3. **Interrupt Harm** — Respond safely when assumptions become teasing, exclusion, or bullying.
4. **Create Belonging** — Design spaces and activities where different people can participate and be valued.
