1. **Knowledge** — Accurate understandable information supports informed choices.
2. **Skills** — Practice turns intention into usable ability.
3. **Social Support** — Encouragement, modeling, accountability, and practical help can sustain change.
4. **Environment** — Availability, defaults, design, time, transportation, and safety shape what is easy.
5. **Incentives** — Rewards, costs, and feedback influence behavior.
6. **Policy** — Rules, budgets, standards, and protections shape opportunity at scale.
