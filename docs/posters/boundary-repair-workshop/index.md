1. **Notice Harm** — Recognize the action, crossed boundary, and effect on the other person.
2. **Take Responsibility** — Name what you did without blaming the other person.
3. **Listen** — Hear the other person’s experience and needs without arguing.
4. **Repair** — Offer a concrete action that addresses the harm when possible.
5. **Rebuild Trust** — Trust returns through consistent behavior over time, if the other person chooses.
