1. **Tummy Signal** — A growling or empty-feeling stomach can be one hunger signal.
2. **Energy and Focus Signal** — Hunger may show as lower energy, headache, irritability, or difficulty concentrating.
3. **Check What the Body Needs** — A pause can distinguish hunger from thirst, tiredness, boredom, or emotion.
4. **Begin Eating with Attention** — Attention to taste, smell, and body comfort makes internal signals easier to notice.
5. **Pause During the Meal** — A midway pause is information, not a rule to stop.
6. **Hunger Decreases** — Hunger usually shifts gradually as food is eaten.
7. **Comfortable Fullness** — Comfortable fullness may feel satisfied, steady, and no longer interested in more food.
8. **Outside Prompt** — Large packages, screens, schedules, and social cues can influence eating even when body signals differ.
9. **Reflect After Eating** — Reflection can help connect choices with wellbeing over time.
