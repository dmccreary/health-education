1. **Notice Signs** — Possible allergy signs can affect skin, eyes, breathing, or digestion.
2. **Stop Exposure** — Move away from the possible allergen and do not eat or touch more.
3. **Get an Adult** — Tell a trusted, trained adult immediately about possible symptoms.
4. **Emergency Help** — Trouble breathing or swelling of the face or throat needs emergency action now.
