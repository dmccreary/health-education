# Three Kinds of Health Defense — Image Generation Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: wide interactive educational poster base for Grade 4
Primary request: Create a clear three-part health-defense comparison showing that infectious disease prevention, non-infectious disease risk reduction, and allergy management need different responses.
Scene/backdrop: A grand school health-defense center divided into three equal shield-shaped bays.
Required text (verbatim, headings only): "INFECTIOUS", "NON-INFECTIOUS", "ALLERGIES"
Subject details: INFECTIOUS shows germ pathways gently blocked by handwashing, covered cough, fresh-air, staying home when ill, safe food, and vaccine shield symbols; NON-INFECTIOUS shows asthma/diabetes/heart-support symbols with sleep, movement, balanced food, medical check-ins, and ongoing management, clearly not contagious; ALLERGIES shows label checking, avoiding known allergens, telling an adult, school safety plans, and urgent help for breathing trouble using non-alarming visual symbols.
Style/medium: polished science-center infographic for children, dimensional shield architecture, crisp, reassuring, evidence-focused.
Composition/framing: 1536×1024 wide landscape, three equal vertical columns, bold heading centered at top, strong separation but unified visual system.
Color palette: teal for infectious, violet for non-infectious, amber for allergies.
Text constraints: Render exactly the three quoted headings, spelled correctly, and no other words anywhere.
Avoid: diagnosis, graphic symptoms, implying non-infectious conditions are anyone's fault, stigmatizing allergies, numbers, arrows, callout marks, brands, watermarks.
