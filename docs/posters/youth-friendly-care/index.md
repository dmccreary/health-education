1. **Rights and Privacy** — Youth-friendly care explains privacy, consent, records, and any limits clearly.
2. **Culturally Safe Care** — Respectful care makes room for identity, language, culture, family, and lived experience.
3. **Sexual Health** — Youth-friendly sexual healthcare is factual, nonjudgmental, inclusive, and private within applicable rules.
4. **Mental Health** — Mental-health care can include screening, counseling, treatment, crisis support, and referrals.
5. **Access Supports** — Transportation, cost, hours, disability access, language, telehealth, and navigation affect access.
