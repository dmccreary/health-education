Use **Explore** mode to select a section and learn more. Use **Quiz Me** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Pause** — Stop long enough for your thinking brain to catch up with your tapping finger.
2. **Check Privacy** — Look for personal information and get permission from anyone shown.
3. **Consider Impact** — Think about how the message could affect everyone who may see it.
4. **Choose** — Send, revise, save, delete, or ask a trusted adult—there is more than one smart choice.
