1. **Notice Pressure** — Pressure may use urgency, guilt, threats, flattery, secrecy, or repeated requests.
2. **State a Boundary** — Use a clear statement such as no, stop, or I am not sharing that.
3. **Save Evidence** — Preserve relevant messages, usernames, dates, or screenshots when safe.
4. **Block or Report** — Use platform, school, or device controls to reduce contact and report abuse.
5. **Get Support** — Tell a trusted adult, school professional, caregiver, or appropriate authority.
