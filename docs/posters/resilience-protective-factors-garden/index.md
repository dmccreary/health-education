1. **Safe Relationships** — Reliable caring relationships provide connection, perspective, and practical help.
2. **Coping Skills** — Flexible coping skills help manage emotion, stress, and difficult situations.
3. **Cultural Roots** — Culture, identity, language, spirituality, and community can strengthen meaning and belonging.
4. **Sleep and Rest** — Rest supports regulation, learning, immunity, and recovery.
5. **Movement** — Movement can support mood, sleep, connection, and physical health.
6. **Meaningful Goals** — Purpose and realistic goals can organize effort and hope.
7. **Healthcare** — Accessible respectful healthcare supports prevention, treatment, and recovery.
8. **Peer Support** — Peers can offer understanding, companionship, and lived-experience wisdom.
9. **School and Work Support** — Flexible policies, accommodations, mentorship, and fair opportunity strengthen stability.
10. **Stable Housing** — Safe stable housing supports sleep, routines, care, and reduced stress.
11. **Community Resources** — Food, transportation, recreation, care, and mutual aid expand practical options.
12. **Hope and New Growth** — Hope can coexist with grief, uncertainty, and slow progress.
