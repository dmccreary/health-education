1. **Sleep** — Sleep supports attention, learning, mood regulation, and physical recovery.
2. **Exercise** — Regular movement supports brain health, mood, sleep, and stress regulation.
3. **Alcohol** — Alcohol can impair judgment, coordination, memory, and reaction time.
4. **Nicotine** — Nicotine is addictive and can affect attention, learning, mood, and impulse control.
5. **Cannabis** — Cannabis can affect attention, memory, coordination, and judgment.
6. **Medication Misuse** — Using medication differently than directed or using someone else’s medicine can cause serious harm.
