1. **Notice** — Body clues can signal that something feels wrong, confusing, or unwanted.
2. **Say No** — A strong, clear “No” or “Stop” communicates a boundary.
3. **Move Away** — Moving toward a safer place or other people creates distance.
4. **Tell a Trusted Adult** — Tell a trusted adult what happened and how you feel.
