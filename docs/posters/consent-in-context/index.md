1. **Clear Communication** — Consent is a clear, voluntary, informed, and specific agreement.
2. **Ambiguity** — Silence, freezing, uncertainty, or mixed signals are reasons to pause.
3. **Withdrawal** — A person can change their mind at any time.
4. **Power Difference** — Authority, age, dependence, status, or fear can limit free choice.
5. **Digital Context** — Sharing messages or images requires permission and respect for privacy.
6. **Legal Context** — Laws vary by age, relationship, activity, and jurisdiction.
