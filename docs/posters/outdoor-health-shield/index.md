Use **Explore** mode to select a section and learn more. Use **Quiz Me** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Sun** — Sun protection reduces skin and heat risks during outdoor time.
2. **Loud Sound** — Very loud sound can hurt hearing even when it is temporary.
3. **Chemicals** — Household and garden chemicals require adult control and secure storage.
4. **Water** — Clear-looking natural water is not automatically safe to drink.
5. **Germs** — Shared outdoor equipment and coughs can move germs between people.
