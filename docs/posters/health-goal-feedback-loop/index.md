Use **Explore** mode to select a marker or section and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Assess** — Notice the current situation, strengths, needs, resources, and constraints.
2. **Clarify Values** — Connect the goal to what matters personally.
3. **Set the Goal** — Define a specific, meaningful, realistic result and review point.
4. **Choose Small Actions** — Break the goal into practical next steps.
5. **Build Support** — Identify people, services, tools, and settings that can help.
6. **Track Observations** — Record useful signals such as completion, energy, barriers, mood, function, or context.
7. **Reflect** — Review what worked, what did not, and why.
8. **Adapt Around Barriers** — Change timing, steps, supports, environment, or the goal when barriers appear.
9. **Revise or Continue** — Continue, expand, pause, or choose a new goal based on evidence and values.
