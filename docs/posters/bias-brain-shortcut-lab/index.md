1. **Notice the Snap Judgment** — Recognize the quick assumption before treating it as fact.
2. **Pause** — Slow the move from assumption to decision.
3. **Check Evidence** — Ask what you actually know and what you inferred.
4. **Seek Perspective** — Listen to people affected and consider viewpoints beyond your own.
5. **Choose Fair Action** — Make a choice that protects dignity, access, and consistent standards.
