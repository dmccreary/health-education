Use **Explore** mode to select a marker or section. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Post** — A post can be copied, interpreted, and seen beyond its intended moment.
2. **Comment** — Comments contribute to a lasting social and data trail.
3. **Like and React** — Reactions can signal approval and shape what systems show next.
4. **Location Clues** — Photos and settings can reveal routines or location even without a location tag.
5. **Copied Screenshots** — Deleted content may survive as screenshots or downloads.
6. **Expanded Audience** — Friends can forward content to audiences you did not choose.
7. **Data and Algorithms** — Platforms may use behavior to predict interests and shape recommendations.
8. **Future Context** — A post may be interpreted differently later or by a new audience.
9. **Privacy and Reporting** — Use privacy, blocking, and reporting controls intentionally.
10. **Trusted Support** — Tell a trusted adult when contact, threats, exploitation, or content feels unsafe.
