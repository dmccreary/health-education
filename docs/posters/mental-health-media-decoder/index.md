Use **Explore** mode to select a marker or section and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Representation** — Ask who is shown, who is missing, and whether the portrayal is complex or stereotyped.
2. **Language** — Notice labels, jokes, fear framing, person-first or identity-first preferences, and tone.
3. **Evidence** — Separate dramatic storytelling, opinion, promotion, and verified health information.
4. **Impact** — Consider how the message may affect stigma, hope, help-seeking, fear, or misunderstanding.
5. **Better Message** — Redesign the message to be accurate, humane, inclusive, and actionable.
