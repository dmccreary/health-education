1. **Learn** — Technology can support learning when a trusted adult approves the activity.
2. **Play** — Healthy technology use balances screen play with movement, rest, and people.
3. **Private Information** — Names, addresses, schools, phone numbers, and photos should not be shared without permission.
4. **Ask an Adult** — When unsure, stop and bring the device to a trusted adult.
