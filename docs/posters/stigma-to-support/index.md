1. **Notice Stigma** — Stigma appears in jokes, labels, exclusion, blame, or assumptions about mental health.
2. **Check the Assumption** — Ask what evidence supports the belief and whose experience is missing.
3. **Use Respectful Language** — Use the words a person prefers and avoid turning a condition into an insult.
4. **Connect to Help** — Offer practical connection to a trusted adult, counselor, clinician, or crisis resource.
5. **Build Belonging** — Inclusive routines, activities, and relationships reduce isolation.
