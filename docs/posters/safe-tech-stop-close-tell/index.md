Use **Explore** mode to select a column and learn about the action. Use **Quiz Me** to practice choosing the correct step for a technology-safety situation.

The three steps are:

1. **Stop** — pause and do not reply.
2. **Close** — leave the app or step away from the device.
3. **Tell** — get help from a trusted adult right away.

Teachers and editors can open [calibration mode](./main.html?edit=true) to adjust the rectangular zone boundaries and copy the updated JSON.
