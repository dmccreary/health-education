# Safe Tech: Stop, Close, Tell — Image Generation Prompt

## Image specifications

- **Format:** PNG
- **Dimensions:** 1536 × 1024 px, wide landscape, 3:2 ratio
- **Audience:** Grade 2 students, ages 7–8
- **Style:** Ultra-high-quality modern children's educational illustration; sophisticated editorial infographic; softly dimensional digital gouache with crisp silhouettes, rich harmonious colors, gentle natural expressions, and polished professional spacing

## Composition

Create a calm, reassuring educational infographic that teaches a child what to do when something online feels unsafe, confusing, or uncomfortable.

A single title band occupies the top 13% of the canvas. Below it are three equal-width vertical columns with generous margins and visible separation:

- Left column: approximately 2–32% of the canvas width
- Center column: approximately 34–66% of the canvas width
- Right column: approximately 68–98% of the canvas width
- Column content: approximately 15–96% of the canvas height

## Exact text

At the top, render **“SAFE TECH: STOP, CLOSE, TELL”** verbatim in large, bold, exceptionally legible dark-navy uppercase type.

Render only these three column headings below it:

- Left: **“STOP”**
- Center: **“CLOSE”**
- Right: **“TELL”**

No other words or letters may appear anywhere in the image.

## Column artwork

### Stop

Use a warm amber and coral palette. Show a diverse elementary-age child seated with a tablet. The screen displays a vague, non-scary unfamiliar pop-up made only of abstract shapes, with no readable text. The child pauses, takes both hands away from the device, and looks thoughtful rather than frightened.

### Close

Use a calm blue and teal palette. Show the same child safely closing or turning away from the app, with one clear, familiar X-shaped close icon integrated into the device interface. Do not show messages, URLs, usernames, or other text.

### Tell

Use a reassuring green and purple palette. Show the child bringing the tablet to a trusted adult teacher or caregiver, who kneels at the child's level and listens calmly. Their body language communicates safety, trust, and teamwork.

## Critical constraints

- Text must be perfectly spelled and rendered exactly as requested.
- Do not add captions, speech bubbles, UI words, labels, numbers, arrows, callout lines, markers, logos, branding, signatures, or watermarks.
- Do not include frightening imagery, shame, punishment, a sinister adult, or dramatic danger.
- Do not imply that the child should investigate or respond to the content.
- Keep the three columns as large rectangular visual regions suitable for invisible interactive hover zones.
- Preserve clear gutters between columns and align the three headings at the same height.
- Keep important faces and device details away from the outer 2% edge.
