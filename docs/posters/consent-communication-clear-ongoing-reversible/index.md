1. **Clear Agreement** — Clear consent is voluntary, specific, and comfortable.
2. **Checking In** — Consent is ongoing, so respectful people check in as circumstances change.
3. **Uncertainty** — Unclear, hesitant, frozen, or mixed responses mean stop.
4. **Withdrawal** — A person can change their mind and withdraw consent at any time.
5. **Coercion** — Threats, guilt, persistence, secrecy, or power pressure invalidate consent.
