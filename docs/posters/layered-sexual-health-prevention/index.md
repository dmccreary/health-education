1. **Consent and Communication** — Clear consent, boundaries, testing conversations, and shared decisions are foundational.
2. **Barrier Methods** — Barrier methods can reduce some STI and pregnancy risks when used correctly and consistently.
3. **Contraception** — Contraceptive methods differ in effectiveness, duration, side effects, access, and user control.
4. **Testing and Vaccination** — Testing can detect infections without symptoms, and vaccination prevents selected infections.
5. **Care and Follow-Up** — Treatment, partner notification support, repeat testing, and ongoing care reduce harm.
