# Image-generation prompt

Annotation-free 1536×1024 compassionate watercolor peer-support journey with twelve distinct scenes from noticing change through listening, direct safety questions, adult and professional help, urgent response, boundaries, follow-up, self-care, and a support team; no text, labels, arrows, self-harm imagery, brands, or watermark.

The final asset was generated at 1536 × 1024 pixels for a wide-landscape interactive overlay.
