## What to draw

Germs are normally invisible, but for teaching purposes show a few tiny, friendly, translucent turquoise-and-purple sparkle-like germ characters clustered near transfer points. They should look like soft rounded specks, not monsters.

## Layout notes

- Arrange the targets as a continuous visual story flowing generally left to right.
- Preserve at least 6–8 percentage points between target centers.
- Keep important targets away from the outer 3% edge.
- Make the healthy habits bright and reassuring without implying that other children are bad.
- Do not use arrows, trails, dividers, panels, or labels.
- Blank any classroom posters and book covers.
- Do not include masks, illness distress, medical equipment, fear, disgust, grime, green slime, shame, ominous lighting, logos, signatures, or watermarks.
