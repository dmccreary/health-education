1. **Scene Safety** — Check for hazards before approaching.
2. **Check Responsiveness** — Tap and shout as taught to see whether the person responds.
3. **Activate Emergency Response** — Call emergency services or direct a specific person to call.
4. **Retrieve the AED** — Send someone to get an AED while help is activated.
5. **Hand Position** — Use the hand position taught in current certified training.
6. **Chest Compressions** — High-quality compressions help circulate blood until advanced care arrives.
7. **Open the AED** — Turn on or open the AED and follow its spoken and visual prompts.
8. **Place Pads** — Place pads exactly as the diagrams on the pads show.
9. **Clear for Analysis** — Make sure no one touches the person while the AED analyzes.
10. **Shock If Advised** — Deliver a shock only when the AED instructs it.
11. **Resume CPR** — Resume compressions promptly after analysis or a shock as directed.
12. **Professional Handoff** — Continue until the person responds, the scene becomes unsafe, or trained responders take over.
