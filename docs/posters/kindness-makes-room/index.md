1. **Notice** — Kindness often starts by noticing how someone else may be feeling.
2. **Invite** — A simple invitation can open a place in the group.
3. **Play Together** — Including others means everyone has a real way to contribute.
4. **Help Repair** — Mistakes can become kind moments when people help fix what happened.
