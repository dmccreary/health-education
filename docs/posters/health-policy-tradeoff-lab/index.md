Use **Explore** mode to select a marker or section. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Goal** — Define the health problem, desired outcome, population, and decision authority.
2. **Evidence** — Assess quality, relevance, uncertainty, feasibility, and alternatives.
3. **Who Benefits** — Examine how benefits and burdens are distributed across groups and places.
4. **Possible Harms** — Anticipate stigma, exclusion, privacy loss, cost, displacement, or unintended behavior.
5. **Revise and Monitor** — Track implementation, outcomes, equity, and unintended effects over time.
