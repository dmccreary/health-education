1. **Notice** — Noticing is the first step: pay attention to who may need connection.
2. **Imagine** — Pause to consider how the situation might feel from another point of view.
3. **Ask and Listen** — A respectful question and genuine listening replace guesses with understanding.
4. **Include and Act** — Use what you learned to include, support, or connect someone with help.
