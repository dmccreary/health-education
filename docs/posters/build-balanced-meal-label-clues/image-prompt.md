# Build a Balanced Meal from Label Clues — Image Generation Prompt

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: annotation-free wide interactive Grade 4 poster base
Primary request: Create a gorgeous market-to-table panorama that supports twelve interactive callout markers about using food-label clues to build a balanced meal.
Scene/backdrop: Left third is a friendly neighborhood market shelf and produce stand; center is a kitchen prep counter; right third is a dining table with a varied balanced meal. The visual flows naturally from choosing to preparing to eating.
Subject details and positions: at 8%/30% a simplified blank nutrition-facts-style panel on a cereal package with black rules but absolutely no writing; 17%/62% whole-grain bread; 27%/33% colorful fresh produce; 36%/70% beans and lentils; 46%/30% plain yogurt or calcium-rich alternative; 54%/67% nuts and avocado; 64%/30% water pitcher; 72%/67% whole-grain bowl; 80%/32% cooked fish and tofu protein choices; 88%/68% vegetables and fruit filling a generous part of the table; 94%/32% a small sweet treat beside the meal; 58%/48% child and trusted adult comparing two packages thoughtfully.
Style/medium: exceptional children's editorial realism, painterly gouache with crisp food detail, culturally inclusive foods, warm home lighting.
Composition/framing: 1536×1024 wide landscape, clear spacious objects at the listed positions, foreground table does not hide market clues, enough open visual space for markers.
Critical constraints: absolutely NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO TITLE, NO LABELS, NO ARROWS, NO CALLOUT LINES, NO ANNOTATION MARKS anywhere. Any package panels must be blank decorative shapes only.
Avoid: exact portion prescription, food moralizing, brands, medical claims, clutter, watermark.
