1. **Carbohydrates** — Carbohydrates are the body’s first source of quick, usable energy.
2. **Protein** — Protein supplies building blocks for growth and repair.
3. **Fats** — Fats provide long-lasting energy, protection, warmth, and vitamin absorption.
4. **Vitamins and Minerals** — Micronutrients are needed in small amounts for many specialized body jobs.
5. **Water** — Water supports nearly every body system.
