1. **What Happened?** — Start with the event: describe what happened without blaming.
2. **What I Feel** — Name the feeling and notice clues in the body.
3. **What I Can Say** — Use a calm I-statement to share the feeling and need.
4. **Who Can Help?** — A trusted adult can listen when words are hard or a problem continues.
