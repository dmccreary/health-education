1. **Wash Hands First** — Clean hands keep germs from moving onto ingredients and tools.
2. **Rinse Produce** — Rinsing removes soil and some germs before food is cut or eaten.
3. **Separate Raw Food** — Raw meat can carry germs that should not reach ready-to-eat food.
4. **Separate Produce Board** — Separate tools help prevent cross-contamination.
5. **Cook Thoroughly** — Thorough cooking destroys many harmful germs.
6. **Use a Clean Plate** — Cooked food stays safer when it never returns to a raw-food plate.
7. **Cool in Shallow Containers** — Shallow containers help leftovers cool more evenly before refrigerator storage.
8. **Store Safely in the Refrigerator** — Cold storage slows germ growth, and sealed lower-shelf raw foods reduce drips.
9. **Pack with an Ice Pack** — Cold foods need to stay cold on the way to lunch.
10. **Pack Clean Utensils and Water** — Clean reusable items prevent yesterday’s food and germs from returning.
11. **Wash Before Eating** — Clean hands protect the food at the final step before eating.
12. **Ask About Questionable Food** — Food that smells strange, looks spoiled, or was stored unsafely should not be tasted.
