1. **Body Clock** — The circadian system helps coordinate sleepiness and alertness across the day.
2. **Light and Screens** — Bright evening light can delay signals that support sleep.
3. **Schedule and Stress** — Irregular schedules, heavy demands, and worry can disrupt sleep.
4. **Caffeine and Substances** — Caffeine and other substances can alter alertness and sleep quality.
5. **Sleep Environment** — Light, sound, temperature, comfort, safety, and interruptions affect sleep.
