Use **Explore** mode to select a numbered marker and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Trusted Starting Point** — A trusted adult can help identify options, transportation, questions, and support.
2. **School Support** — School health staff can answer questions, provide support, and connect students with care.
3. **Primary Care Clinic** — Primary care supports prevention, common health concerns, chronic conditions, and referrals.
4. **Telehealth** — Telehealth can reduce distance barriers for some kinds of care.
5. **Pharmacy** — Pharmacists can explain medication use, interactions, storage, and disposal.
6. **Adolescent and Sexual Health Care** — Youth-friendly clinics may offer development, relationship, STI, pregnancy-prevention, and reproductive-health services.
7. **Mental Health Care** — Mental-health professionals support stress, mood, relationships, trauma, and safety concerns.
8. **Transportation** — Transportation can determine whether a service is truly reachable.
9. **Language Access** — Interpreters and communication supports help patients understand and be understood.
10. **Cost and Coverage Support** — Support staff can explain coverage, sliding fees, and assistance programs.
11. **Disability Access** — Physical, sensory, cognitive, and communication access are part of quality care.
12. **Follow-Up and Navigation** — Care navigation helps schedule referrals, results, treatment, and follow-up.
