1. **Water** — Water is the best everyday choice for replacing fluid.
2. **Nourishing Drinks** — Plain milk and culturally familiar unsweetened alternatives can add nutrients as well as fluid.
3. **Sugary Drinks** — Some drinks contain a large amount of added sugar even when they look fruity or sporty.
4. **Caffeinated Drinks** — Caffeine can affect a child’s sleep, heart rate, and calm focus.
