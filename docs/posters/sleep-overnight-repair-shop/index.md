1. **Wind-Down Signals** — A predictable wind-down helps the brain and body shift toward sleep.
2. **Consistent Timing** — Similar sleep and wake timing helps the body organize its daily rhythm.
3. **Memory Sorting** — During sleep, the brain organizes and strengthens learning and memories.
4. **Emotion Reset** — Sleep helps emotion systems recover and improves the ability to pause before reacting.
5. **Growth and Repair** — Sleep supports growth signals and tissue repair in a growing body.
6. **Immune Support** — Sleep supports immune defenses that help the body respond to illness.
7. **Energy Recharge** — Rest restores alertness and physical energy for the next day.
8. **Temperature Rhythm** — Body temperature naturally shifts as part of the sleep-wake rhythm.
9. **Morning Attention** — Adequate sleep supports attention, decision-making, and learning.
10. **Sleep Environment** — A safe, quiet, comfortable sleep space supports rest.
