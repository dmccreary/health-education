Use **Explore** mode to select a marker or section and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Verify Information** — Check authorship, evidence, methods, date, conflicts, and agreement across credible sources.
2. **Map Influences** — Health behavior is shaped by individual, relationship, community, system, and policy influences.
3. **Compare Options** — Compare likely benefits, harms, feasibility, equity, cost, and unintended effects.
4. **Set a Goal** — Define a specific, meaningful, measurable, and realistic result.
5. **Tailor the Message** — Match language, channel, imagery, and call to action to the audience.
6. **Evaluate Results** — Measure reach, quality, outcomes, equity, and unintended effects.
