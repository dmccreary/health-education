Use **Explore** mode to select a numbered marker and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Serving Information** — Serving information tells what amount the rest of a label describes.
2. **Servings in the Container** — A package can contain more than one serving.
3. **Food Energy** — Food energy supports movement, growth, thinking, and body work.
4. **Fats** — The fats line reports the amount in one serving.
5. **Carbohydrates** — The carbohydrate line reports the amount in one serving.
6. **Added-Sugar Clue** — Sugar information helps compare sweetened foods and drinks.
7. **Protein** — The protein line reports the amount in one serving.
8. **Caffeine Clue** — Caffeine may interfere with sleep, heart rate, and calm focus in children.
9. **School-Day Snack Context** — A useful school snack fits the time available, storage conditions, and the student’s needs.
10. **Active Afternoon or Long Outing** — Longer activity or time away may change hydration, storage, and energy needs.
