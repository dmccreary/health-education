Use **Explore** mode to select a numbered marker and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Choose One Goal** — A useful personal goal is specific, realistic, and connected to something the student values.
2. **Take a Small First Action** — Small repeatable actions make a goal easier to begin.
3. **Invite a Helper** — Trusted adults, family, and friends can remind, model, listen, or make time.
4. **Adjust Around Obstacles** — Obstacles are information, not proof that a goal failed.
5. **Check Progress** — A check-in asks what happened, what helped, and what needs adjustment.
6. **Notice Feelings** — Goals can bring mixed feelings. Naming them helps students respond rather than quit automatically.
7. **Learn from Positive Models** — Positive models show what a healthy action can look like.
8. **Celebrate and Choose the Next Step** — Celebration recognizes effort, learning, and support—not just a perfect finish.

Teachers and editors can open [calibration mode](./main.html?edit=true) to drag markers and copy updated coordinates.
