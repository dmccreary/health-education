# Health Information Detective Desk — Image Generation Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: wide interactive educational poster base for Grade 3
Primary request: Create a magnificent detective desk comparing five kinds of health-information sources.
Scene/backdrop: A top-down evidence desk arranged as five large equal case-file stations, playful but sophisticated, with a magnifying glass and evidence motifs.
Required text (verbatim, headings only): "TRUSTED ADULT", "HEALTH PROFESSIONAL", "PUBLIC HEALTH", "AD OR INFLUENCER", "ANONYMOUS POST"
Subject details: TRUSTED ADULT has a family-and-teacher photo icon and open conversation; HEALTH PROFESSIONAL has stethoscope and clinic reference book; PUBLIC HEALTH has a civic building, reviewed report, and science symbols; AD OR INFLUENCER has a bright product spotlight, price tag, and phone camera; ANONYMOUS POST has an unsigned message card, question mark silhouette, and missing-source clues. Across stations visually suggest author, evidence, purpose, date, and fit using symbols only.
Style/medium: museum-quality editorial infographic illustration for children, tactile paper files, realistic desk textures, cinematic overhead light, crisp friendly headings.
Composition/framing: 1536×1024 wide landscape, five equal vertical file zones with clear boundaries and uncluttered top headings.
Color palette: forest green, blue, teal, orange, and plum over warm wood.
Text constraints: Render exactly the five quoted headings, spelled correctly, and no other words anywhere.
Avoid: real agencies, brands, social media logos, readable claims, numbers, arrows, annotation marks, watermarks.
