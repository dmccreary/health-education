Use **Explore** mode to select a section and learn more. Use **Quiz Me** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Trusted Adult** — A trusted adult knows the child and can help decide what deserves another check.
2. **Health Professional** — Training and professional accountability make clinicians strong sources within their expertise.
3. **Public Health Source** — Official public-health sources review evidence for guidance that serves communities.
4. **Ad or Influencer** — Attention and sales motives mean the claim needs independent checking.
5. **Anonymous Post** — Missing authorship and evidence make an anonymous health post hard to verify.
