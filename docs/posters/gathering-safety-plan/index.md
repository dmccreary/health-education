1. **Plan with a Trusted Adult** — Share where you will be, who is present, and how to get help.
2. **Charge the Phone** — A charged phone supports contact, navigation, and emergency calls.
3. **Use a Buddy Check-In** — Friends can agree to check in and leave together.
4. **Know the Exits** — Notice safe exits, adult hosts, and calmer spaces.
5. **Keep Personal Items in Sight** — Keep beverages, medication, and belongings under your control.
6. **Refuse Pressure** — Use a clear refusal, repeat it, change location, or involve support.
7. **Choose a Sober Ride** — Never ride with an impaired driver.
8. **Help a Sick Friend** — Stay nearby when safe and involve an adult.
9. **Call Emergency Help** — Unresponsiveness, breathing trouble, seizure, serious injury, or immediate danger require emergency services.
10. **Check In Afterward** — Confirm a safe return and talk about any concerning event.
