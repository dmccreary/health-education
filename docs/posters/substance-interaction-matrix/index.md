1. **Alcohol** — Alcohol can impair judgment, coordination, memory, and breathing at high exposure.
2. **Nicotine** — Nicotine can increase heart rate, affect attention and mood, and produce dependence.
3. **Cannabis** — Cannabis can affect memory, attention, coordination, judgment, and anxiety.
4. **Stimulants** — Stimulants can increase alertness, heart rate, temperature, anxiety, and sleep disruption.
5. **Opioids** — Opioids can relieve pain but can also slow breathing and cause dependence or overdose.
6. **Medications** — Medicines have intended effects, side effects, and interaction risks.
