1. **Notice Who Is Left Out** — Upstander action begins by noticing exclusion, bullying, or rising tension.
2. **Invite and Include** — A direct invitation can reduce exclusion and create belonging.
3. **Interrupt Calmly** — When it is safe, a brief statement can name the problem and interrupt it.
4. **Distract or Redirect** — A distraction can break a harmful moment without escalating it.
5. **Move Toward Safety** — Help the targeted person reach a safer place or group.
6. **Get a Trusted Adult** — Adults have responsibility and authority to respond to bullying and possible violence.
7. **Document Safely** — Documentation may help only when school policy allows and it does not increase risk.
8. **Support Afterward** — Private support after an incident can reduce isolation.
9. **Report Possible Fighting** — Warning signs of a possible fight should be reported before anyone is hurt.
10. **Follow Up and Restore Belonging** — Check in later and help rebuild a welcoming environment.
