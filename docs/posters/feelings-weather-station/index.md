1. **Happy** — Happy can feel light, warm, energetic, or smiley. Naming it helps us share joy.
2. **Sad** — Sad may come with tears, low energy, or wanting comfort. A trusted adult can listen and help.
3. **Angry or Frustrated** — Anger and frustration are strong feelings. Safe choices include pausing, breathing, and using words.
4. **Worried** — Worry can make a tummy feel jumpy or thoughts race. Naming the worry can make help easier to find.
5. **Excited** — Excitement can bring fast energy, big smiles, and lots of movement.
6. **Scared** — Fear can warn us to seek safety. Moving near and telling a trusted adult is a strong choice.
7. **Calm** — Calm may feel steady, relaxed, and ready to think. Slow breathing can help the body settle.
8. **Lonely or Left Out** — Feeling left out can hurt. A trusted adult can listen and help make room for belonging.
