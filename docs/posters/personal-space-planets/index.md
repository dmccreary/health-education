1. **Ask Before Getting Close** — Permission helps a person choose whether a hug, touch, or close game feels welcome.
2. **Notice a Step Back** — Stepping back, turning away, or becoming quiet can signal a need for more space.
3. **Mutual Play Feels Different** — Playful interaction is shared fun when everyone is participating willingly.
4. **Stop When the Signal Changes** — A game that began as fun should stop when someone looks uncomfortable or says stop.
5. **Space Needs Vary** — People prefer different distances depending on the person, place, activity, and day.
6. **Make Room to Move** — Respectful space includes room for bodies, mobility devices, and safe movement.
7. **Interrupt Teasing** — Teasing is not playful when it embarrasses, excludes, or continues after discomfort.
8. **Check In and Repair** — After a boundary mistake, listening and changing the behavior are part of repair.
9. **Get Trusted Help** — Adult help is appropriate when teasing continues, safety is involved, or words feel hard.
