1. **Label Evidence** — Labels provide structured evidence about a packaged food.
2. **Marketing Claims** — Marketing shapes attention and expectations, but it is not neutral evidence.
3. **Cultural Guidance** — Family and cultural traditions carry knowledge, identity, taste, and patterns of balance.
4. **Food Access** — Cost, transportation, time, availability, storage, and kitchens shape practical choices.
5. **Food Sovereignty** — Food sovereignty concerns communities’ power to shape their own food systems.
