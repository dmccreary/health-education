1. **Check the Scene** — Pause long enough to notice hazards before approaching.
2. **Recognize** — Look for unresponsiveness, breathing trouble, severe bleeding, choking, or other urgent signs.
3. **Call for Help** — Activate a trusted adult and emergency services quickly.
4. **Choking Response** — Severe choking requires immediate trained action and emergency activation.
5. **Emergency Medication** — Emergency medication should follow the person’s plan and trained adult guidance.
