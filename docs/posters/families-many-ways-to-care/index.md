Use **Explore** mode to select a numbered action. Use **Quiz** mode to find the act of care named in each question.

The eight callouts are:

1. Making a Place Together
2. Sharing Food and Traditions
3. Listening to a Story
4. Sun Safety Together
5. Comfort and Listening
6. Everyone Joins the Game
7. Offering Water
8. A Warm Welcome

Teachers and editors can open [calibration mode](./main.html?edit=true) to drag markers and copy updated coordinates.
