1. **Accurate Information** — Use medically accurate, age-appropriate sources rather than rumors.
2. **Boundaries and Consent** — Clear, voluntary, ongoing consent and respected boundaries support safety.
3. **Barrier Methods** — Barrier methods can reduce some infection and pregnancy risks when used correctly.
4. **Testing and Care** — Testing and timely care help identify and treat infections.
5. **Trusted Support** — Trusted adults and qualified professionals can help with questions, access, and safety.
