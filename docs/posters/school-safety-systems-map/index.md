1. **Welcoming Climate** — Belonging, respect, and predictable routines support prevention and reporting.
2. **Trusted Reporting** — Students need clear, accessible ways to report concerns without retaliation.
3. **Mental Health Support** — Counselors and referral pathways support early help and crisis response.
4. **Accessible Design** — Entrances, routes, alarms, information, and procedures must include people with varied disabilities.
5. **Emergency Communication** — Clear, redundant communication helps people act during changing conditions.
6. **Trained Staff** — Staff need role-specific practice for prevention, first aid, evacuation, shelter, and reunification.
7. **First Aid and AED** — Accessible supplies and trained responders improve emergency readiness.
8. **Evacuation** — Evacuation plans use safe routes, accountability, accessibility, and alternate options.
9. **Shelter Procedures** — Some hazards require sheltering rather than leaving.
10. **Reunification** — A reunification plan connects students with authorized caregivers safely and accurately.
11. **Digital Safety** — Secure systems, privacy practices, reporting, and media literacy reduce digital harm.
12. **Student Voice** — Students notice barriers and can improve plans, climate, and communication.
