Use **Explore** mode to select a numbered marker and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea in the illustration.

1. **Castle Climb** — Climbing builds muscles, grip, coordination, and confidence.
2. **Ribbon Dance** — Dancing moves the whole body and can lift mood and release stress.
3. **Rolling Obstacle Course** — Wheeling quickly can build endurance, steering skill, and confidence.
4. **Animal Stretches** — Stretching gently practices flexibility, balance, focus, and calm breathing.
5. **Team Ball Pass** — Cooperative ball play builds coordination, attention, and teamwork.
6. **Stepping-Stone Jumps** — Jumping develops leg strength, balance, and body control.
7. **Low-Log Balance** — Balancing trains core muscles, focus, and steady movement.
8. **Helmeted Scooters** — Scooting develops leg strength, coordination, and endurance.
9. **Water and Recovery** — Rest and hydration help the body recover after active play.

Teachers and editors can open [calibration mode](./main.html?edit=true) to drag markers and copy updated coordinates.
