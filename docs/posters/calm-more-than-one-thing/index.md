1. **Breathing** — Slow breathing can give the body and thinking brain a moment to settle.
2. **Movement** — Walking, stretching, dance, sport, or other movement can help release tension.
3. **Art and Music** — Drawing, making rhythm, singing, or listening can give feelings a safe form.
4. **Nature** — Outdoor time can create sensory space for reflection and grounding.
5. **Connection** — Supportive people, shared traditions, prayer, reflection, and community can restore steadiness.
