1. **Sleep Changes** — Stress can change sleep length, timing, or quality.
2. **Body Tension** — Headaches, tight muscles, or stomach discomfort can accompany stress.
3. **Racing Thoughts** — Worry can make thoughts feel fast, repetitive, or hard to pause.
4. **Pulling Away** — Withdrawing from usual people or activities can signal distress.
5. **Concentration Difficulty** — Stress can make schoolwork and decisions harder.
6. **Mood Changes** — Irritability, sadness, or feeling numb may be stress signals.
7. **Restorative Practice** — Movement, rest, art, breathing, or time outdoors can support regulation.
8. **Cultural Healing** — Cultural traditions and community practices can strengthen coping and belonging.
9. **Peer Listening** — A peer can listen, validate, and help connect someone to support.
10. **Trusted Adult** — A trusted adult can help assess needs and take next steps.
11. **Professional Support** — Counselors and health professionals can offer assessment and treatment.
12. **Urgent Crisis Help** — Immediate danger or thoughts of suicide require urgent adult and emergency support.
