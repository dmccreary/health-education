1. **Endurance** — Endurance helps the heart and lungs support activity over time.
2. **Strength** — Muscular strength is the ability of muscles to produce force.
3. **Flexibility** — Flexibility is the comfortable range of motion around joints.
4. **Balance and Coordination** — Balance controls body position; coordination organizes movements together.
