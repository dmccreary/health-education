# Pressure Escape Route — Image Generation Prompt

Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: annotation-free wide interactive Grade 4 poster base
Primary request: Create an empowering branching school-and-community panorama for practicing situational awareness, peer-pressure refusal, safe exits, and help-seeking.
Scene/backdrop: A continuous after-school route begins at a school courtyard on the left, passes a park and corner shop in the center, and reaches a community center and home area on the right. The scene is ordinary and calm, not frightening.
Subject details and positions: 8%/32% child noticing body signals and pausing; 18%/70% group inviting a risky shortcut; 30%/32% visible open school office with trusted staff; 40%/68% clear public path away from pressure; 50%/30% two peers with one calmly using a direct no gesture; 60%/70% another child suggesting a safer alternative activity; 70%/30% well-lit community-center entrance; 80%/70% phone used to contact a trusted adult; 90%/32% supportive adult listening; 94%/73% safe home destination. Use visual branching paths through landscaping and architecture, not drawn arrows.
Style/medium: beautiful narrative editorial illustration for upper elementary students, cinematic watercolor-gouache, diverse children, confident body language, emotionally nuanced.
Composition/framing: 1536×1024 wide landscape, ten distinct marker-friendly focal points, clear spacing, panoramic storytelling.
Critical constraints: absolutely NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO TITLE, NO LABELS, NO ARROWS, NO CALLOUT LINES, NO ANNOTATION MARKS anywhere.
Avoid: depicting actual danger, weapons, substances, threatening adults, victim blame, dramatic fear, logos, watermarks.
