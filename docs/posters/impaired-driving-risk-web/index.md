1. **Substance Impairment** — Alcohol, cannabis, some medicines, and other drugs can impair driving.
2. **Attention** — Impairment can narrow, divide, or disrupt attention.
3. **Reaction Time** — Impairment can slow the time between noticing a hazard and responding.
4. **Judgment** — Impairment can increase confidence while reducing accurate risk assessment.
5. **Passenger Influence** — Passengers can increase pressure or help stop an unsafe trip.
6. **Seatbelts** — Every occupant should use a seatbelt on every trip.
7. **Alternate Ride** — Use a sober driver, caregiver, taxi or rideshare, transit, or a safe place to stay.
8. **Hold the Keys Safely** — A sober friend or adult may help prevent access to a vehicle when it can be done safely.
9. **Community and Legal Consequences** — Impaired driving can harm people and bring school, family, financial, and legal consequences.
10. **Emergency Response** — Call emergency services for a crash, unresponsive person, breathing danger, or immediate threat on the road.
