Use **Explore** mode to select a marker or section and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Family and Culture** — Families and cultures shape routines, meanings, knowledge, food, care, and support.
2. **Peers** — Peers can model norms, provide belonging, share information, or apply pressure.
3. **School** — School policies, curriculum, climate, meals, activity, care, and belonging affect health.
4. **Media** — Media shape attention, norms, emotion, identity, and perceived choices.
5. **Healthcare** — Care affects prevention, diagnosis, treatment, trust, and navigation.
6. **Neighborhood** — Design, safety, housing, recreation, and social connection affect daily opportunity.
7. **Transportation** — Transportation determines which opportunities are reachable and at what cost in time and money.
8. **Food Access** — Price, availability, quality, culture, time, and transportation shape food choices.
9. **Policy** — Rules and budgets shape products, environments, care, education, work, and safety.
10. **Economic Resources** — Income, wealth, work conditions, and time affect housing, food, care, rest, and stress.
11. **Natural Environment** — Air, water, heat, climate, green space, and hazards affect health.
12. **Community Advocacy** — Organized community voice can identify priorities, build solutions, and shift power.
