Use **Explore** mode to select a marker or section. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Mutual Respect** — People value each other’s dignity, choices, identity, time, and goals.
2. **Open Communication** — People can speak honestly, listen, and repair misunderstandings.
3. **Boundaries** — Clear limits are communicated and respected without punishment.
4. **Concerning Patterns** — Jealousy, repeated pressure, monitoring, humiliation, or isolation are warning signs.
5. **Control** — Threats, coercion, stalking, forced access, isolation, or violence are unsafe.
6. **Get Support** — Trusted people and specialized services can help assess options and safety.
