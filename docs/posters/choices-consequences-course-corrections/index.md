Use **Explore** mode to select a numbered marker and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Name the Goal** — A clear goal gives decisions a direction.
2. **Notice Influences** — Family, peers, culture, media, emotions, and access can shape a choice.
3. **Compare Options** — List more than one realistic option before deciding.
4. **Short-Term Consequences** — Short-term consequences happen soon after a choice.
5. **Long-Term Consequences** — Long-term consequences may affect future health, relationships, habits, or opportunities.
6. **Use Support** — Trusted people and resources can improve a decision.
7. **Reflect Honestly** — Reflection asks what happened, what helped, and what needs adjustment.
8. **Change Course** — A new route can be chosen when evidence or circumstances change.
9. **Own the Next Step** — Ownership means choosing the next realistic action and tracking it.
