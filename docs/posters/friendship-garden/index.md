Use **Explore** mode to select a numbered marker and learn more. Use **Quiz** mode to practice finding each idea in the illustration.

1. **Share Tools** — Healthy friends share materials and make sure both people can participate.
2. **Listen with Attention** — Listening shows that a friend’s ideas and feelings matter.
3. **Welcome Someone New** — A friendly welcome helps a new person feel that they belong.
4. **Value Different Ideas** — Friends can like different plants, games, foods, and ideas and still care for each other.
5. **Ask for Space** — Healthy boundaries let people say when they need more room or quiet.
6. **Take Turns** — Taking turns gives each person a fair chance to contribute.
7. **Repair a Mistake** — Everyone makes mistakes. Healthy friends notice harm, apologize, and help repair it.
8. **Adapt So Everyone Joins** — Belonging means changing the activity so every person has a meaningful way to join.
9. **Celebrate a Friend** — Healthy friends can feel happy about another person’s effort and success.
10. **Check In and Comfort** — Checking in can sound like “Are you okay?” or “Would you like company?”
