1. **Freely Given Yes** — Consent is a clear, voluntary, comfortable agreement without pressure.
2. **Uncertain or Paused** — Hesitation or mixed signals mean stop and check in.
3. **Clear Boundary** — No, stop, or a changed mind must be respected immediately.
4. **Pressure or Coercion** — Pressure, guilt, threats, secrecy, or misuse of power are not consent.
