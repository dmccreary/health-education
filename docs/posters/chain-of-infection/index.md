1. **Pathogen** — A pathogen is a microorganism or agent capable of causing disease.
2. **Reservoir** — A reservoir is where the pathogen normally lives or multiplies, such as people, animals, or environments.
3. **Exit** — A portal of exit is how a pathogen leaves its reservoir.
4. **Transmission** — Transmission may be direct, airborne, droplet, surface, food, water, blood, or vector-borne.
5. **Entry** — A portal of entry is how the pathogen reaches a new host.
6. **Susceptible Host** — Susceptibility reflects immunity, exposure, health, age, vaccination, and social conditions.
