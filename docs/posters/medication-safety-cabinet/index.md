1. **Read the Label** — Read the medicine label and directions with a trusted adult when appropriate.
2. **Correct Person** — Use only medicine intended for the named person.
3. **Correct Timing** — Follow the prescribed or labeled schedule.
4. **Check Allergies and Interactions** — Medicines can interact with allergies, other drugs, supplements, alcohol, or health conditions.
5. **Ask a Professional** — Pharmacists and clinicians can explain purpose, use, storage, and side effects.
6. **Secure Storage** — Store medicine away from children, pets, and people for whom it was not prescribed.
7. **Original Container** — Keep medicine in its original labeled container unless a professional-approved organizer is used.
8. **Do Not Share** — Do not offer or accept prescription medicine.
9. **Safe Disposal** — Use a take-back location or local disposal guidance for unused medicine.
10. **Poison or Emergency Help** — Call poison control or emergency services for a suspected overdose, severe reaction, or dangerous mistake.
