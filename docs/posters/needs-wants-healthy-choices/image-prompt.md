# Needs, Wants & Healthy Choices — Image Generation Prompt

Please generate a 1536 × 1024 px wide-landscape Kindergarten infographic in polished digital-gouache style.

## Exact text

Render only these exact strings:

- **NEEDS, WANTS & HEALTHY CHOICES**
- **MY BODY NEEDS**
- **THINGS I WANT**
- **A HEALTHY CHOICE**

No other words, letters, or numbers may appear.

## Layout

Use a title band across the top 12%, followed by three equal vertical columns:

1. **My Body Needs** — blue and teal; water, nourishing foods, pillow and blanket, helmet, comfortable shoes, and a trusted adult giving a child a warm side hug.
2. **Things I Want** — yellow and coral; stuffed animal, blocks, art supplies, small dessert, game controller, and a smiling child. Keep this panel equally warm and positive: wants are fun, not bad.
3. **A Healthy Choice** — green and violet; a child packs water and nourishing food, puts on a helmet, and chooses a simple handwashing goal card while a trusted adult encourages them.

Keep each column rectangular with clear gutters for invisible interactive zones. Use diverse characters, gentle expressions, and crisp object silhouettes.

## Constraints

No extra text, captions, speech bubbles, product labels, logos, numbers, arrows, callout marks, checklists, scales, money, price tags, shame, scolding, red X marks, or moral ranking. Do not depict wants as unhealthy or forbidden. Do not imply children make major decisions without trusted-adult help.
