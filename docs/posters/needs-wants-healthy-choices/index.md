[Open the interactive poster full screen](./main.html){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

Use **Explore** mode to select a column and learn about it. Use **Quiz Me** to practice recognizing needs, wants, and healthy choices.

1. **My Body Needs** — what bodies require for health, safety, and care.
2. **Things I Want** — enjoyable things that are not required for health.
3. **A Healthy Choice** — one small action that supports health or safety.

Teachers and editors can open [calibration mode](./main.html?edit=true) to adjust the rectangular zone boundaries and copy the updated JSON.
