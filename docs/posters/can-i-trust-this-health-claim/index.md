Use **Explore** mode to select a section and learn more. Use **Quiz Me** mode to practice finding each idea.

1. **Evidence** — Ask whether the claim is supported by checkable research or only a dramatic story.
2. **Expertise** — Check whether the source has relevant training and can be held accountable.
3. **Sales Motive** — Notice who benefits if you believe the claim or buy the product.
4. **Recency** — Check when information was published or updated and whether guidance may have changed.
5. **Personal Fit** — Ask whether the advice fits the person, family, culture, condition, and situation.
