# Can I Trust This Health Claim? — Image Generation Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: wide interactive educational poster base for Grade 4
Primary request: Create a dazzling five-stage health-claim testing studio where students examine a fictional product claim critically.
Scene/backdrop: A friendly science-and-media studio divided into five equal testing bays. A fictional glowing wellness gadget travels through each bay without any brand or readable claim.
Required text (verbatim, headings only): "EVIDENCE", "EXPERTISE", "SALES MOTIVE", "RECENCY", "PERSONAL FIT"
Subject details: EVIDENCE compares a research report with a testimonial speech bubble using symbols; EXPERTISE checks the qualifications and accountability of a source; SALES MOTIVE reveals a price tag and spotlight behind the claim; RECENCY uses calendar and update symbols to show that dates matter; PERSONAL FIT shows a child consulting a trusted adult or health professional because needs and context differ.
Style/medium: premium retro-futurist educational infographic, richly dimensional lab instruments, clear icons, exciting but intellectually serious.
Composition/framing: 1536×1024 wide landscape, five equal vertical bays with distinct machinery, exact heading centered high in each.
Color palette: cobalt, teal, amber, magenta, and green over deep navy.
Text constraints: Render exactly the five quoted headings, spelled correctly, and no other words anywhere.
Avoid: real products, miracle language, testimonials with readable text, brands, medical diagnosis, numbers, arrows, callout marks, watermarks.
