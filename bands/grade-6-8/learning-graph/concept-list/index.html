    Concept List — Grade 6-8¶

    This list expands the 59 benchmark-coded topics, 19 Skill Standards benchmarks, and 26 key-vocabulary terms in the Grade 6-8 Course Description into individual concept nodes. Every concept below traces back to a specific benchmark or vocabulary term in that document. A small number of bridging concepts (e.g., "Health", "Trusted Adult", "Consent") are carried forward from the K-5 bands to connect related concepts across strands. Compound benchmarks — common at this band because standards frequently bundle several ideas into one statement — are split into natural sub-concepts.
