    Course Description Quality Assessment — Grade 6-8¶

    Source Document¶

    docs/bands/grade-6-8/course-description.md

    Summary¶

    The Grade 6-8 course description is the most extensive and structurally mature document produced so far in this series. It is derived from the 2025 Minnesota K-12 Health Academic Standards and enumerates 59 benchmark-coded topics across six content strands (Food and Nutrition; Human Growth, Development, and Sexual Health; Mental and Emotional Health; Personal Health and Wellness; Personal Safety and Violence Prevention; and the entirely new Substance Use Awareness and Prevention strand) plus a large cross-cutting Skill Standards group (19 benchmarks). This is nearly double the topic count of Grade 5 (31 benchmarks), consistent with Grade 6-8 being graded as a three-grade span rather than a single grade.

    This assessment evaluates the description against a right-sized concept target of approximately 120-135 concepts (not the skill's generic 200-concept default), reflecting a proportional scale-up from the K-5 bands while avoiding invented content not grounded in the standards.

    Strengths¶

    • Every topic traces to a specific MDE benchmark code, giving the learning graph strong provenance — 59 topic benchmarks plus 19 Skill Standards benchmarks (78 total), roughly double Grade 5's 31.

    • Largest and most granular topic set of the series, reflecting the shift to a 3-grade span (6, 7, 8) rather than a single grade.

    • Explicitly documents the two structurally new elements at this band — Sexual Health folded into Human Growth and Development, and Substance Use Awareness and Prevention as a brand-new strand — with clear rationale (state mandate effective 2026-2027) and clear signaling that no K-5 bridging exists for these two areas.

    • Strongest combined Analyze/Understand tiers in the series (21 outcomes each), matching the standards' own repeated "apply skill cues to analyze" phrasing.

    • Strongest Create tier of the series (5 outcomes vs. Grade 5's 1), reflecting the band's shift toward designing workout plans, meal plans, goal plans, and health messages.

    • Detailed "Bridging Concepts for Students New to This Band" section explicitly separates content that carries forward from K-5 (nutrition labels, consent/boundaries, emotions/mental health, bullying/bystander action, fitness, refusal skills, goal-setting) from content that is genuinely new at this band (Sexual Health, Substance Use).

    • Cross-references Appendix A (statewide-mandated topics: vaping, cannabis/ fentanyl, STI/HIV/HPV prevention) which land substantially in this band, strengthening traceability to the primary standards document.

    Gaps¶

    • No dedicated "Topics Excluded" section (partial credit given based on related context in the Reading Level & Cognitive Demand section).

    • Remember-level outcomes are limited to 3, thinner than Understand/Analyze — but this is an accurate reflection of the standards' own emphasis on higher-order cognitive demand at this band, not a document defect.

    • Evaluate-level outcomes (2) are the thinnest Bloom's tier in this document, though Analyze-level outcomes at this band frequently blend in evaluative language ("analyze and evaluate a thoughtful decision-making process") which somewhat compensates.

    Concept Count Guidance¶

    Per explicit run-level override, this band targets approximately 120-135 concepts (not 200), built from the 59 enumerated topic benchmarks, the 19 Skill Standards benchmarks, the 26 Key Vocabulary terms, and a small number of clearly-implied bridging/connective concepts carried forward from Grade 5. This roughly doubles Grade 5's 70-concept graph, proportional to the near-doubling of benchmark count (31 to 59 topic benchmarks) and the addition of a wholly new strand (Substance Use Awareness and Prevention).

    Overall Quality Score: 92 / 100¶

    This score is above both the skill's general 70-point proceed threshold and its 80-point "good quality" guidance, and is the highest score in this series to date. Proceeding automatically with learning graph generation per run authorization (unattended run — no user pause required).
